### 3-2 — CONTRADICTS-NODE — severity: high

**The field says** (`trace[0].family`): "金属在酶里只有三种当法：它本身就是活性中心的一部分；当一座桥，把底
物架到酶上；**或者当电子的中转站**。被问到「金属在这儿起什么作用」，从这三个里挑。"

**The node says** (`points[4]`): "Metal ions function in the enzyme molecule in THREE ways: as
part of the ACTIVE CENTRE; as BRIDGES for binding the substrate to the enzyme; or as an element
**STABILISING THE SPATIAL STRUCTURE** of the enzyme molecule." — and `terms` ("metalloenzyme …
the metal acts in the active centre, as a substrate bridge, or as a **structure stabiliser**")
and `quiz[4]`, whose `accept` list is `["active centre", "bridge", "stabilis", "stabiliz",
"structure", "活性中心", "桥", "稳定"]` and whose model answer ends "or as an element stabilising
the spatial structure of the enzyme molecule."

**Why these cannot both hold:** the node states a closed list of exactly three functions, and
the `trace` states the same closed list with a different third member. It is not a paraphrase —
"electron relay" and "stabilises the spatial structure" are different claims, and the trace adds
"只有三种" and an instruction to answer from *its* three. A student who studies the trace and is
then asked the node's own short question will produce an answer containing none of the accepted
keywords for the third slot. Metals do act as electron relays in real biochemistry (the node
even lists cytochrome Fe among its metalloenzymes), which is exactly why this reads as plausible
and would not be caught by a word-overlap scan.

**Resolvable from the node alone?** Yes — `points[4]`, `terms` and the quiz all agree against the
trace, and p.43 is recorded as verified against the scan.

### 3-3-1 — LANGUAGE (notation) — severity: low

You asked specifically whether every field of `3-3-1` holds one notation. **The substrate/product
notation is clean throughout** — `A` and `X` everywhere, `[A]` in both the Lineweaver–Burk

- `mustKnow`: "V_max (this book's V_lim)" ✔
- `terms` V_lim: "elsewhere written V_max" ✔

Three are unreconciled, and all three are in the Cornish-Bowden & Eisenthal material, which
suggests one passage written from a different source:

- `points[9]`: "the COORDINATES OF THE INTERSECTION OF THE ASYMPTOTES of this hyperbola are
  precisely K_m and **V_max**. (Obr. 3.4 — the direct linear plot, with −K_m on the [A] axis and
  **V_lim** on the v axis.)" — both symbols in one point, one sentence apart
- `terms`, Cornish-Bowden entry: "…give K_m and **V_max** directly"
- `quiz[3].answer_en` / `answer_cn`: "…are precisely K_m and **V_max**" / "…就是 K_m 与 **V_max**"

**Why this is low and not medium:** V_lim and V_max are the same quantity, so nothing false is
learned. It is listed because the node itself makes the symbol an exam point ("worth
memorising"), and then prints the other symbol in an answer key — a reader may reasonably wonder
whether the asymptote method yields something different from the Lineweaver–Burk intercept.
`oral` is clean (says "the maximal rate"), and `gapPoints` is clean (V_lim throughout).

**Resolvable from the node alone?** Yes — three one-word edits.
