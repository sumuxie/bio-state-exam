## Kapitola 1: Obecné zákonitosti živých soustav (生命系统的普遍规律)

### 1.1 Co je je biochemie? (什么是生物化学？)

#### 【捷克语教材原文 (CZ Text)】
> *Biochemie je věda studující chemickou podstatu života, chemické složení živých organismů a chemické děje, které v nich probíhají.*  
> 生物化学是研究生命的化学本质、生物体的化学组成以及其中发生的化学过程的科学。

#### 【中文深度解析 (CN In-Depth Explanation)】
1. **研究对象**：生物化学结合了生物学与化学，旨在在分子水平（Molecular Level）解释生物现象。
2. **三个基本支柱**：
   * **静态生化 (Static Biochemistry)**：研究生物分子的结构（如蛋白质、核酸、糖类、脂质）。
   * **动态生化 (Dynamic Biochemistry)**：研究代谢途径（Metabolic Pathways）以及能量转换（Bioenergetics）。
   * **功能/分子生物学 (Functional Biochemistry)**：遗传信息的传递（复制、转录、翻译）。

#### 【英语口试表达与专业术语 (EN Oral Exam Prep)】
* **Key Vocabulary**: Molecular level, Biopolymers (生物大分子), Metabolic pathways, Bioenergetics, Information transfer.
* **Q: How would you define biochemistry and its core branches during an oral exam?**
  > **Model Oral Answer**: *"Biochemistry is the fundamental science that investigates the chemical basis of life. It bridges chemistry and biology to explore living systems at the molecular level. It is traditionally divided into structural biochemistry—focusing on biomolecules like proteins and nucleic acids; metabolic biochemistry—studying chemical transformations and energy transduction; and molecular genetics—explaining genetic information storage and expression."*

### 1.2 Živé a neživé (生物与非生物的区别)

#### 【捷克语教材原文 (CZ Text)】
> *Živé soustavy se od neživých liší vysokým stupněm uspořádanosti, schopností metabolismu, autoreprodukce, reakce na podněty a evoluce. Jsou to otevřené termodynamické systémy s konstantním proudem hmoty a energie.*

#### 【中文深度解析 (CN In-Depth Explanation)】
1. **开放热力学系统 (Open Thermodynamic Systems)**：
   * 生物体必须与外界环境持续交换**物质 (Matter)** 和 **能量 (Energy)**。
   * **定态 (Steady State / Homeostasis)**：不同于化学平衡（Equilibrium, 死寂状态），活着意味着保持在远离热力学平衡的高度的稳态。
2. **负熵 (Negative Entropy)**：
   * 薛定谔提出“生命靠吸收负熵维持”。生物体通过消耗自由能（ATP/食物）将系统内部的熵（混乱度）维持在较低水平。

#### 【英语口试表达与专业术语 (EN Oral Exam Prep)】
* **Key Vocabulary**: Open system, Thermodynamic equilibrium, Homeostasis (内环境稳态), Negative entropy (负熵), Steady state.
* **Q: Why are living organisms described as open thermodynamic systems away from equilibrium?**
  > **Model Oral Answer**: *"Living organisms are open thermodynamic systems because they continuously exchange both matter and energy with their environment. Unlike non-living systems that spontaneously move toward thermodynamic equilibrium and maximum entropy, living organisms maintain a dynamic steady state—or homeostasis—far from equilibrium. They consume free energy from nutrients to perform biological work and export entropy to their surroundings, sustaining high internal organization."*

### 1.3 Stavební kameny živé hmoty (生命的构建基块：从分子到细胞)

#### 【捷克语教材原文 (CZ Text)】
> *Základní prstky živé hmoty (biogenní prvky): H, C, N, O, P, S tvoří přes 98 % hmotnosti organismu. Monomery (aminokyseliny, monosacharidy, nukleotidy) se polymerují na biopolymery (proteiny, polysacharidy, nukleové kyseliny).*

#### 【中文深度解析 (CN In-Depth Explanation)】
1. **造生元素 (Biogenic Elements)**：
   * **主要元素 (Macroelements)**：C, H, O, N, P, S（构成蛋白质、核酸、糖和脂质的共价骨架）。
   * **离子/电解质**：$\text{Na}^+, \text{K}^+, \text{Ca}^{2+}, \text{Mg}^{2+}, \text{Cl}^-$（维持渗透压、膜电位与酶辅因子）。
   * **微量元素 (Microelements)**：Fe, Cu, Zn, Mn, Co, Mo, Se（常作为酶活性中心的金属辅因子）。
2. **生物层次结构 (Hierarchy of Life)**：
   * 无机前体 ($\text{H}_2\text{O}, \text{CO}_2, \text{NH}_3$) $\rightarrow$ 代谢中间体 $\rightarrow$ 构建单体 (Monomers) $\rightarrow$ 生物大分子 (Biopolymers) $\rightarrow$ 超分子复合物 (Supramolecular Complexes) $\rightarrow$ 细胞器与细胞。

#### 【英语口试表达与专业术语 (EN Oral Exam Prep)】
* **Key Vocabulary**: Biogenic macroelements, Trace elements (微量元素), Monomer, Polymer, Supramolecular assembly.
* **Q: Outline the hierarchical organization of biomolecules from elemental components to functional cells.**
  > **Model Oral Answer**: *"The molecular organization of life follows a strict hierarchy. It begins with biogenic macroelements—primarily carbon, hydrogen, oxygen, nitrogen, phosphorus, and sulfur—which combine to form simple precursor molecules. These precursors build fundamental biological monomers: amino acids, monosaccharides, fatty acids, and nucleotides. Monomers covalently polymerize into macromolecules such as proteins, nucleic acids, and polysaccharides. These macromolecules associate non-covalently into supramolecular complexes like ribosomes and membranes, ultimately organizing into subcellular organelles and intact cells."*

## Kapitola 2: Aminokyseliny a proteiny (氨基酸与蛋白质)

### 2.1 Aminokyseliny (氨基酸的结构、性质与化学反应)

#### 【捷克语教材原文 (CZ Text)】
> *Standardní aminokyseliny v proteinech jsou L-α-aminokyseliny s obecným vzorcem R-CH(NH2)-COOH. Izoelektrický bod pI je hodnota pH, při které má aminokyselina nulový čistý náboj (zwitterion).*

#### 【中文深度解析 (CN In-Depth Explanation)】
1. **结构与手性 (Structure & Stereochemistry)**：
   * 20种标准蛋白氨基酸除 Glycine (甘氨酸) 外均含有手性 C 原子，自然界中蛋白质均由 **L-构型** 氨基酸组成。
2. **两性解离与等电点 ($pI$)**：
   * 氨基酸在水溶液中以两性离子（Zwitterion）形式存在。
   * 当 $\text{pH} < pI$ 时，分子带正电，向阴极移动；当 $\text{pH} > pI$ 时，分子带负电，向阳极移动。
   * 计算公式：
     * 中性/疏水氨基酸：\( pI = \frac{pK_{a1}(\text{COOH}) + pK_{a2}(\text{NH}_3^+)}{2} \)
     * 酸性氨基酸 (Asp, Glu)：\( pI = \frac{pK_{a1} + pK_{aR}}{2} \)
     * 碱性氨基酸 (Lys, Arg, His)：\( pI = \frac{pK_{a2} + pK_{aR}}{2} \)
3. **重要鉴定反应**：
   * **茚三酮反应 (Ninhydrin Reaction)**：与 $\alpha$-氨基酸生成蓝紫色复合物（脯氨酸 Pro 显示黄色），用于定量测定。
   * **桑格反应 (Sanger's Reaction - DNFB)**：DNFB 与 N-末端氨基酸结合，用于一级结构测序。
   * **Edman 降解 (Edman Degradation - PITC)**：苯异硫氰酸酯可循环切下 N-末端残基而不破坏其余肽链。

#### 【英语口试表达与专业术语 (EN Oral Exam Prep)】
* **Key Vocabulary**: Zwitterion, Isoelectric point ($pI$), Chiral center, L-configuration, Ninhydrin reaction, Edman degradation, Amphoteric.
* **Q: Explain the concept of the isoelectric point (pI) of amino acids and how it dictates their charge behavior at varying pH.**
  > **Model Oral Answer**: *"The isoelectric point, or $pI$, is defined as the specific pH at which an amino acid carries no net electrical charge, existing predominantly as a dipolar zwitterion. At a pH below its $pI$, the functional groups accept protons, giving the molecule a net positive charge. Conversely, at a pH above its $pI$, the groups donate protons, yielding a net negative charge. Calculating $pI$ depends on the pKa values of the ionizable groups: for uncharged side-chains, it is the average of the carboxyl and amino pKa values; for acidic or basic amino acids, it is calculated from the two pKa values that flank the neutral zwitterionic form."*

### 2.2 Proteiny (蛋白质的结构层次、构象与变性)

#### 【捷克语教材原文 (CZ Text)】
> *Primární struktura je sekvence aminokyselin spojených peptidovou vazbou. Sekundární struktura (α-helix, β-list) je stabilizována vodíkovými můstky hlavního řetězce. Terciární struktura představuje 3D prostorové uspořádání. Denaturace je ztráta nativní konformace bez štěpení peptidových vazeb.*

#### 【中文深度解析 (CN In-Depth Explanation)】
1. **肽键的性质 (Peptide Bond)**：
   * 具有双键性质（共振杂化），不可自由旋转，具有平面结构（Peptide Plane），通常为反式（trans）构型。
2. **二级结构 (Secondary Structure)**：
   * **$\alpha$-螺旋 ($\alpha$-helix)**：右手螺旋，每圈含有 **3.6 个氨基酸残基**，螺距 0.54 nm。主链上的 $\text{C=O}$ 与下方的 $\text{N-H}$ 形成平行于螺旋轴的氢键。
   * **$\beta$-折叠 ($\beta$-sheet)**：肽链呈伸展状态，相邻链间靠垂直于链方向的氢键连接，分为平行（Parallel）与反平行（Anti-parallel，更稳定）。
3. **三级与四级结构 (Tertiary & Quaternary Structure)**：
   * 三级结构依赖侧链（R基团）相互作用：**疏水作用（最主要驱动力）**、盐键（离子键）、二硫键（Cys共价键）、氢键。
   * 四级结构由多个非共价连接的亚基（Subunits）组成。
4. **蛋白质变性 (Denaturation)**：
   * 受加热、强酸强碱、重金属或尿素作用，**破坏二、三、四级结构（非共价键及二硫键）**，但**不破坏一级结构（肽键未断裂）**。变性后生物活性丧失，溶解度降低。

#### 【英语口试表达与专业术语 (EN Oral Exam Prep)】
* **Key Vocabulary**: Primary sequence, Peptide bond plane, $\alpha$-helix (3.6 residues/turn), $\beta$-pleated sheet, Hydrophobic collapse, Denaturation, Renaturation.
* **Q: Distinguish between protein tertiary and quaternary structures, and explain what happens during denaturation.**
  > **Model Oral Answer**: *"The tertiary structure of a protein describes the overall three-dimensional folding of a single polypeptide chain, stabilized by non-covalent interactions—such as hydrophobic interactions in the interior, hydrogen bonds, and ionic salt bridges—as well as covalent disulfide bonds. Quaternary structure, on the other hand, exists only in oligomeric proteins and refers to the spatial arrangement and interaction of multiple independent polypeptide subunits. Denaturation is the process wherein a protein loses its native, biologically active three-dimensional conformation due to external stress like heat, extreme pH, or chaotropic agents. Crucially, denaturation disrupts secondary, tertiary, and quaternary structures without cleaving the covalent peptide bonds of the primary sequence."*

## Kapitola 3: Enzymy (酶与酶动力学)

### 3.1-3.3 Klasifikace, struktura a kinetika enzymů (酶的分类、结构与米氏动力学)

#### 【捷克语教材原文 (CZ Text)】
> *Enzymy jsou biologické katalyzátory snižující aktivační energii (ΔG‡). Michaelisova-Mentenové rovnice: v = (Vmax * [S]) / (Km + [S]). Km udává koncentraci substrátu při v = Vmax/2.*

#### 【中文深度解析 (CN In-Depth Explanation)】
1. **酶国际分类 (EC 6大类)**：
   1. **氧化还原酶 (Oxidoreductases)**：催化氧化还原反应（如脱氢酶 Dehydrogenases）。
   2. **转移酶 (Transferases)**：基团转移（如激酶 Kinases 转移磷酸基团）。
   3. **水解酶 (Hydrolases)**：加水断裂键（如酯酶、蛋白酶）。
   4. **裂合酶 (Lyases)**：非水解消除断裂或形成双键（如脱碳酶 Decarboxylases）。
   5. **异构酶 (Isomerases)**：分子内异构化（如异构酶、变位酶 Mutases）。
   6. **连接酶/合成酶 (Ligases/Synthetases)**：消耗 ATP 连接两分子。
2. **米氏动力学推导与意义 (Michaelis-Menten Kinetics)**：
   * 方程：\( v = \frac{V_{max} \cdot [S]}{K_m + [S]} \)
   * **$K_m$ (米氏常数)**：反应速度达到 $\frac{1}{2} V_{max}$ 时的底物浓度 $[S]$（单位：$\text{mol/L}$）。
   * **$K_m$ 的生物学意义**：反映酶与底物的亲和力。**$K_m$ 越小，亲和力越高**。
   * **$V_{max}$**：酶被底物完全饱和时的最大反应速度。
   * **$k_{cat}$ (催化常数 / 转换数 Turnover Number)**：\( k_{cat} = \frac{V_{max}}{[E]_T} \)，表示单位时间内每个酶分子转换底物的分子数。
   * **催化效率 (Catalytic Efficiency)**：\( \frac{k_{cat}}{K_m} \)，衡量酶在低底物浓度下的催化性能。

#### 【英语口试表达与专业术语 (EN Oral Exam Prep)】
* **Key Vocabulary**: Active site, Coenzyme (辅酶), Prosthetic group (辅基), Activation energy ($\Delta G^\ddagger$), Michaelis constant ($K_m$), Turnover number ($k_{cat}$), Catalytic efficiency ($k_{cat}/K_m$).
* **Q: Derivation and physiological meaning of Km and Vmax in Michaelis-Menten kinetics.**
  > **Model Oral Answer**: *"Enzymes accelerate chemical reactions by lowering the activation energy barrier ($\Delta G^\ddagger$) without altering the overall thermodynamic equilibrium. In Michaelis-Menten kinetics, the initial reaction rate $v$ is related to substrate concentration $[S]$ by the equation $v = \frac{V_{max}[S]}{K_m + [S]}$. $V_{max}$ is the theoretical maximum velocity achieved when all enzyme active sites are fully saturated with substrate. $K_m$, the Michaelis constant, equals the specific substrate concentration at which the reaction rate reaches half of $V_{max}$. Physiologically, $K_m$ acts as an inverse measure of enzyme-substrate affinity; a low $K_m$ indicates strong binding affinity. To evaluate total enzymatic capability, we use the catalytic efficiency ratio, $k_{cat}/K_m$, which reflects how effectively an enzyme binds and converts substrate at physiological concentrations."*

### 3.5 Inhibice enzymových reakcí (酶抑制作用：竞争性、非竞争性与反竞争性)

#### 【捷克语教材原文 (CZ Text)】
> *Inhibice: 1. Kompetitivní (inhibitor soupeří o aktivní místo; Km se zvyšuje, Vmax se nemění). 2. Nekompetitivní (vazba na alosterické místo; Km se nemění, Vmax se snižuje). 3. Ankompetitivní (vazba pouze na komplet ES; Km i Vmax se snižují).*

#### 【中文对比分析表 (CN Comparative Table)】

| 抑制类型 (Inhibition Type) | 结合位置 (Binding Site) | 对 $K_m$ 的影响 | 对 $V_{max}$ 的影响 | 林-贝克双倒数图特征 (Line-Weaver Burk Plot) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **竞争性 (Competitive)** | 活性中心 (Active Site) | **增大 ($\uparrow$)** | **不变 (Unchanged)** | 相交于 Y 轴 (Y-intercept identical) |
| **非竞争性 (Non-competitive)** | 别构中心 (Allosteric Site) | **不变 (Unchanged)** | **减小 ($\downarrow$)** | 相交于 X 轴 (X-intercept identical) |
| **反竞争性 (Uncompetitive)** | 仅结合 ES 复合物 | **减小 ($\downarrow$)** | **减小 ($\downarrow$)** | 两条平行线 (Parallel lines) |

#### 【英语口试表达与专业术语 (EN Oral Exam Prep)】
* **Key Vocabulary**: Active site competition, Allosteric binding, Lineweaver-Burk double reciprocal plot, Reversible vs Irreversible inhibition.
* **Q: Compare competitive, non-competitive, and uncompetitive enzyme inhibition using kinetic parameters and Lineweaver-Burk plots.**
  > **Model Oral Answer**: *"Enzyme inhibition can be categorized into three distinct reversible modes based on binding mechanics and kinetic effects. In competitive inhibition, the inhibitor resembles the substrate and competes directly for the active site. Adding excess substrate outcompetes the inhibitor; thus, the apparent $K_m$ increases while $V_{max}$ remains unchanged. On a Lineweaver-Burk double-reciprocal plot, the lines intersect on the Y-axis. In non-competitive inhibition, the inhibitor binds to an allosteric site regardless of whether substrate is bound. This decreases the effective concentration of functional enzyme, reducing $V_{max}$ while leaving substrate affinity ($K_m$) unchanged; on a Lineweaver-Burk plot, lines intersect on the X-axis. Finally, in uncompetitive inhibition, the inhibitor binds exclusively to the enzyme-substrate (ES) complex. This pulls the equilibrium toward ES, decreasing both $K_m$ and $V_{max}$ by the same ratio, producing parallel lines on a Lineweaver-Burk plot."*
