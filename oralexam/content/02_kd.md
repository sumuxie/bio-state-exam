# 卡 02 · What is the dissociation constant (Kd) and how is it defined?

- **题库** Biochemistry #4 · **weight 3**
- **出处** Biochemie OCR p055、p056、p060（书没有单独讲 Kd，它的 Kd 定义藏在 Ki 那一节）
- **兼任** 大本营卡之一。所有别的卡的「留口子」都可以落到这里
- **建立** 2026-09-11

## 这题的特殊之处

**书里没有「disociační konstanta」这个词条。** 全书 grep 只有一处把某个常数明确定义成解离常数：
p060 讲竞争性抑制时定义 **Ki**——

> *"Sílu interakce mezi inhibitorem a enzymem charakterizuje tzv. inhibiční konstanta K<sub>i</sub>
> definovaná jako **disociační konstanta** komplexu EI: K<sub>i</sub> = [E][I] / [EI]"*

所以**书承认的 Kd 定义就是这个形式**：游离的两个 × 分之 复合物。答题时用这个形式，
老师能对上他书里的东西。

---

## 一口气说完（兄弟页 · 漏 2 条以内算过）

1. **定义**：Kd 是**解离反应的平衡常数**，complex ⇌ 两个游离组分。
2. **式子**：Kd = [P][L] / [PL]。书自己的写法是 Ki = [E][I]/[EI]（p060）。
3. **量纲是浓度**，不是无量纲数。所以能跟浓度直接比大小。
4. **数值的意思**：**半数结合位点被占据时的游离配体浓度**。
5. **方向是反的**：Kd 越小，结合越紧。
6. **倒数是 Ka**，association constant。
7. **也等于两个速率常数之比**：Kd = k<sub>off</sub> / k<sub>on</sub>。
8. **它是热力学量**，ΔG° = RT ln Kd。一个数就是一个自由能。
9. **Km 不是 Kd**（书自己前后打架，见 ⑦）。
10. **留口子**：这个数怎么测出来的，取决于有没有真的到平衡。

---

## 段 1 · 定义

> "A dissociation constant is the equilibrium constant of the dissociation reaction: the complex
> falling apart into its two free partners. So it is the free protein times the free ligand,
> divided by the complex. The textbook writes exactly this form for the inhibition constant:
> K i equals free enzyme times free inhibitor over the enzyme–inhibitor complex.
> Because it is a ratio of concentrations over a concentration, it has units of concentration."

**为什么这样开口。**「它是一个平衡常数」这句话把整题的性质定死了：平衡常数属于热力学，
不属于动力学。后面 Km 那个陷阱就是靠这句话挡住的。

---

## 段 2 · general

> "Three things follow from that definition.
> First, since it has units of concentration, it can be compared with a concentration —
> and numerically it is the free-ligand concentration at which half the binding sites are occupied.
> Second, the direction is inverted: a small K d means tight binding, because a small number means
> the complex does not fall apart easily.
> Third, it has a kinetic reading as well — K d equals the off-rate divided by the on-rate.
> The same number can be reached from equilibrium or from the two rate constants."

---

## 段 3 · 稍展开（挑「一半占据」那条推）

> "The half-occupancy reading is worth doing explicitly, because it is what makes K d usable at the
> bench. Put the free ligand concentration equal to K d in the mass-action expression, and the
> concentration of complex comes out equal to the concentration of free protein — so exactly half
> the protein is bound. That is why you can read K d straight off a binding curve as the midpoint,
> without knowing the total protein concentration."

〔可裁〕再往下一层：
> "And because it is an equilibrium constant, it is a free energy in disguise:
> ΔG° equals R T times the natural log of K d. Every tenfold improvement in K d is about
> 5.7 kilojoules per mole at room temperature."

> ✔ **已核**：RT ln10 在 298.15 K = **5.71 kJ/mol**，算过。书里没有这个数，这条来自热力学定义，
> 不是书。被追问出处就说 "that is just R T ln ten"。

## 段 4 · 留口子（这就是大本营）

> "And this is the number I actually measure. I work with yeast display, where you titrate labelled
> antigen against cells and read the midpoint by flow cytometry. The part that decides whether the
> number means anything is whether the titration really reached equilibrium — at low concentrations
> that can take hours, and if you read too early you report a K d that is too weak."

**这一段留了三个口子，全在你的主场**：yeast display、flow cytometry、平衡时间。
老师追哪一个都是你的地盘。

## 定义链（段 1–4 里没交代的词，各一句）

- **equilibrium constant** — The ratio of concentrations that stops changing once the forward and
  reverse rates are equal.
- **mass action** — The rule that a reaction's rate is set by the product of the concentrations
  taking part in it.
- **on-rate / off-rate** — How fast complex forms per unit of each partner, and how fast it falls
  apart once formed.
- **occupancy** — The fraction of binding sites that are filled at a given free-ligand concentration.
- **titration** — Holding one partner fixed and stepping the other across a concentration range.
- **yeast display** — The binding protein is expressed anchored on the yeast surface, so one cell
  carries one variant and flow cytometry reads binding cell by cell.

---

## ④ 为什么（三层，落到地基）

1. **为什么一个数字就能描述一个结合？**
   因为在平衡态，体系只有一个自由度：给定游离配体浓度，占据率就定了。整条结合曲线由一个参数决定。
2. **为什么半数占据的位置就是 Kd？**
   把 [L] = Kd 代回质量作用式，[PL] = [P]。这不是巧合，是 Kd 的定义直接推出来的。
3. **那 Kd 本身由什么决定？**
   由结合前后的自由能差：ΔG° = RT ln Kd。所以 Kd 不是界面的几何性质，是一个**能量**。

**停止规则 · 落在「热力学」**
> "Below that, K d is just a free-energy difference on a concentration scale, and how that free
> energy is partitioned into enthalpy and entropy is calorimetry rather than biochemistry."

**第二条停止线 · 落在「测量限制」**（这条更适合你，因为可以顺势转进主场）
> "There is also a practical floor: a K d is only as good as the equilibrium behind it. Below about
> the concentration of your own binding sites, a titration stops reporting affinity and starts
> reporting how much protein you put in."

## ⑤ 怎么发现的

> ⚠ **书里没有年份和人名，下面这些是从记忆写的，核过再用。**
> Michaelis 与 Menten 1913 的快速平衡处理；Briggs 与 Haldane 1925 的稳态处理。
> 书 p055 用的是**稳态**（*"ustálený stav (angl. steady-state)"*），但**没有点名 Briggs 和 Haldane**。

**书能支撑的说法**（不带人名，安全）：
> "The steady-state treatment is the one this textbook uses: after mixing, the concentration of the
> enzyme–substrate complex first rises and then stops changing, because its rate of formation and
> its rate of breakdown become equal. The whole Michaelis–Menten equation comes out of setting
> those two equal."

| 名字 | 书里给的内容 | 页 |

| **竞争性抑制在 Lineweaver-Burk 图上的表现** | **Vmax 不变，Km 升高** | p060 |
| **竞争性抑制的经典例子** | **malonate 抑制 succinate dehydrogenase**（像底物到能结合，但不能被脱氢） | p060 |
| **Michaelis constant K<sub>m</sub>** | 酶恰好半饱和时的底物浓度；量纲是浓度 | p056 |
| **Km 的组成** | 书把式子**印出来了**：Km = (k₋₁ + k₂) / k₁ ✔ 已回原图确认 | p055 |
| **steady state（ustálený stav）** | EA 浓度先升后恒定，生成速率 = 分解速率 | p055 |
| **两步机制** | E + A ⇌ EA 快且**可逆**，弱相互作用；EA → E + X 慢且**不可逆** | p054 |
| **一级动力学** | [A] ≪ Km 时，速率正比于 [A] | p056 |
| **零级动力学** | [A] ≫ Km 时，速率恒定为 Vmax，酶被底物饱和 | p056 |
| **Lineweaver–Burk** | 直接从 v–[A] 曲线定 Km、Vmax 不精确，实践用这个线性变换 | p056 |
| **活化能** | 催化的本质是换一条活化能更低的路径，不是给分子加能量 | p054 |
| **为什么生物不能靠加热提速** | 生物大多**热不稳定**（thermolabilní） | p054 |

## ⑦ 陷阱（这题最重要的一格）

### 1 · Km 不是 Kd，而且**书自己前后打架**

这是全卡最值钱的一条，因为你可以**引用这本书反驳这本书**，那是最安全的位置。

| 书说了什么 | 在哪 |
|---|---|
| Km *"vyjadřuje **afinitu** enzymu k substrátu"*，Km 越小底物越好 | p056 |
| 但同一段推导里，书**把 Km 印成 (k₋₁ + k₂)/k₁**——那是速率常数的组合，不是平衡常数 ✔ 已回原图确认 | p055 |
| 而书唯一明确定义成**解离常数**的是 Ki = [E][I]/[EI] | p060 |

**真相**：Km = (k₋₁ + k₂)/k₁，而 Kd = k₋₁/k₁。**只有当 k₂ ≪ k₋₁ 时两者才相等。**
催化快的酶，Km 会明显大于 Kd，这时候把 Km 当亲和力读就是错的。

**考场上怎么说**（先给书，再给条件，不跟老师对答案）：
> "The textbook reads K m as an affinity, and that is fair as long as catalysis is slow compared with
> the substrate falling back off. Strictly, K m is k minus one plus k two, over k one, while a true
> dissociation constant is k minus one over k one. They coincide only in the rapid-equilibrium case.
> The clean dissociation constant in this book is K i, which it defines as [E][I] over [EI]."

### 2 · Kd 小 = 结合紧。方向是反的

被问急了最容易说反。记法：Kd 是**拆开**的常数，拆得越难，数越小。

### 3 · Kd 是热力学量，kinetics 是另一回事

kon 和 koff 各自是动力学量，它们的**比值**才是热力学量。
两个 Kd 相同的结合，kon/koff 可以差几个数量级，生物学后果完全不同。

### 4 · 半数占据 ≠ 半数蛋白被用掉

只有在**游离**配体浓度等于 Kd 时才成立。如果蛋白浓度跟 Kd 同量级，加进去的配体大半被吃掉，
游离浓度远低于加入浓度，曲线中点就不再是 Kd。**这是滴定的浓度下限的由来。**

### 5 · Ki 有两个，别混

竞争性的 Ki 是 EI 的解离常数；反竞争性的 Ki′ 是 EAI 的（p060）。书两个都给了。
