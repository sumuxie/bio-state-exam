1 · 蛋白质的结构 四层 · 四种力 · 两个数
Primary structure is the order of residues, held by peptide bonds. It determines all the higher levels.
一级 ＝ 残基顺序，肽键。它决定高级结构（Anfinsen 那件事）。
卡 01
Secondary structure is local main-chain conformation — α-helix, β-sheet, β-turn, random coil — held by hydrogen bonds between backbone C=O and N–H, not side chains.
二级 ＝ 主链局部构象，靠主链自己的氢键（C=O 和 N–H），跟侧链无关。
卡 01
Tertiary structure is the fold of one whole chain. The main force is the hydrophobic interaction, plus ionic bonds, hydrogen bonds and disulfides.
三级 ＝ 一整条链的折叠。最主要的力是疏水作用，其次离子键、氢键、二硫键。
卡 01
Quaternary structure exists only with more than one chain, and it is held by non-covalent forces.
四级 ＝ 多条链才有，靠非共价力。
卡 01
Denaturation destroys the higher-order structure but not the peptide bonds. The protein loses activity, becomes less soluble, and is easier for proteases to cut.
变性 ＝ 高级结构被破坏，一级结构和肽键不破坏。失活、溶解度下降、更易被酶水解。
卡 01
At its isoelectric point a protein has zero net charge, so it does not move in an electric field and its solubility is lowest.
pH ＝ pI 时净电荷为 0，电场里不移动，溶解度最小、最容易沉淀。
卡 01 · aa 卡
Protein is quantified at 280 nm because of the aromatic residues, mainly tryptophan and tyrosine.
280 nm 定量蛋白，靠的是色氨酸和酪氨酸（苯丙氨酸贡献很小）。

2 · 核酸 配对 · 增色 · Tm · 三种 RNA
The backbone is held by 3′,5′-phosphodiester bonds. The double helix is right-handed, and the two strands are antiparallel.
骨架是 3′,5′-磷酸二酯键；双螺旋右手，两条链反平行。
卡 03
A pairs with T by two hydrogen bonds, G with C by three. So the higher the G–C content, the more stable the duplex.
A=T 两个氢键，G≡C 三个氢键。所以 GC 含量越高越稳定。
卡 03
On denaturation the strands separate and the absorbance at 260 nm rises. That is the hyperchromic effect.
变性 → 双链分开 → 260 nm 吸光度升高，叫增色效应。
卡 03
Tm is the temperature at which half the DNA has melted. It rises with G–C content and with the ionic strength of the medium.
Tm ＝ 变性到 50% 时的温度。正比于 GC 含量和介质离子强度。
卡 03
mRNA is about five per cent of the RNA but the most diverse. Eukaryotic mRNA carries a 5′ cap and a 3′ poly-A tail.
mRNA 只占约 5% 但种类最多；真核的有 5′ 帽（防降解、帮翻译起始）和 3′ polyA 尾（维持稳定）。
卡 12 · mb 卡
tRNA is cloverleaf in secondary structure and an inverted L in three dimensions. It carries the anticodon loop and ends in CCA, where the amino acid is attached.
tRNA 二级结构三叶草，三级结构倒 L；有反密码子环，3′ 端是 CCA，氨基酸挂在这儿。

rRNA is the most abundant RNA in the cell, about eighty per cent, and with protein it builds the ribosome.
rRNA 含量最多（约 80%），跟蛋白一起构成核糖体。
卡 12
3 · 酶 Km · 两种抑制 · 辅酶与辅基
An enzyme lowers the activation energy. It speeds up a reaction that is already thermodynamically allowed, and it does not change the equilibrium constant.
酶降低活化能，只加速热力学上允许的反应，不改变平衡常数。
卡 17 · 卡 02
A loosely bound cofactor is a coenzyme and can be removed by dialysis. A tightly or covalently bound one is a prosthetic group and cannot.
结合松的叫辅酶，透析能除去；结合紧或共价的叫辅基，透析除不掉。
卡 18
Km is the substrate concentration at half Vmax. A small Km means high affinity. Km is a property of the enzyme and is independent of enzyme concentration.
Km ＝ 半 Vmax 时的底物浓度。Km 越小亲和力越大；Km 是酶的特征常数，与酶浓度无关。
卡 02
Competitive inhibition: the inhibitor competes for the active site, so Km rises and Vmax is unchanged, and enough substrate overcomes it. Non-competitive: the inhibitor binds elsewhere, so Km is unchanged and Vmax falls.
竞争性：争活性中心 → Km 增大，Vmax 不变，加底物可以克服。非竞争性：结合在别处 → Km 不变，Vmax 降低。
卡 02
Classic examples: malonate on succinate dehydrogenase, and sulfonamides, which compete with PABA in bacterial folate synthesis.
经典例子：丙二酸抑制琥珀酸脱氢酶；磺胺与 PABA 竞争，堵住细菌的叶酸合成。
丙二酸在卡 24 · ⚠ 磺胺 2026-09-27 补
Six classes: oxidoreductase · transferase · hydrolase · lyase · isomerase · ligase.
六大类：氧化还原 · 转移 · 水解 · 裂合 · 异构 · 连接。⚠ 裂合在异构前面。
卡 17
4 · 糖代谢 关键酶 · 账 · PPP 的三个数
Glycolysis runs in the cytosol. Its three irreversible steps are hexokinase (or glucokinase), phosphofructokinase-1, and pyruvate kinase. PFK-1 is the main control point.
糖酵解在胞质；三个不可逆 ＝ 己糖激酶／葡萄糖激酶 · PFK-1 · 丙酮酸激酶，PFK-1 最核心。
卡 gly
Without oxygen one glucose gives two ATP and two lactate. With oxygen, my textbook gives thirty-eight.
无氧：净 2 ATP ＋ 2 乳酸。有氧：书给 38（现代 30–32）。
卡 gly
Pyruvate dehydrogenase complex is irreversible and sits in the mitochondrial matrix. It needs five cofactors: TPP, lipoamide, CoA, FAD and NAD⁺ — four of them from B vitamins.
PDH 不可逆，在线粒体基质；五个辅因子：TPP(B1) · 硫辛酸 · CoA(泛酸) · FAD(B2) · NAD⁺(PP)。
卡 24 · 横向卡
TCA runs in the matrix. Three irreversible steps: citrate synthase, isocitrate dehydrogenase (the key one), and α-ketoglutarate dehydrogenase. One turn gives 3 NADH, 1 FADH₂, 1 GTP and 2 CO₂.
TCA 在线粒体基质；三限速酶 ＝ 柠檬酸合酶 · 异柠檬酸脱氢酶（最关键）· α-KG 脱氢酶。一圈 3 NADH ＋ 1 FADH₂ ＋ 1 GTP ＋ 2 CO₂。
卡 24
PPP runs in the cytosol. Its control enzyme is G6PD, and what it senses is NADP⁺, not ATP. Per glucose-6-phosphate: two NADPH, one CO₂, no ATP either way.
PPP 在胞质，限速酶 G6PD，信号是 NADP⁺ 而不是 ATP。2 NADPH · 1 CO₂ · 0 ATP。

Glycogen: the key enzyme of synthesis is glycogen synthase, of breakdown glycogen phosphorylase. Phosphorylation switches breakdown on and synthesis off.
糖原合成关键酶 糖原合酶，分解关键酶 糖原磷酸化酶；磷酸化让分解开、合成关。
横向卡 · 卡 06
Gluconeogenesis happens mainly in liver and kidney, and four enzymes bypass the irreversible steps: pyruvate carboxylase, PEP carboxykinase, fructose-1,6-bisphosphatase and glucose-6-phosphatase.
糖异生主要在肝和肾；四个绕行酶 ＝ 丙酮酸羧化酶 · PEPCK · 果糖-1,6-二磷酸酶 · 葡糖-6-磷酸酶。

5 · 脂代谢 入口 · 一轮的账 · 酮体
The rate-limiting step of β-oxidation is getting into the mitochondrion, by carnitine palmitoyltransferase 1, and malonyl-CoA inhibits it.
β-氧化的限速步是进线粒体那道门 —— CPT-1，被丙二酰-CoA 抑制。
横向卡速背 11
One round of β-oxidation gives one NADH, one FADH₂ and one acetyl-CoA, and shortens the chain by two carbons. Rounds = carbons ÷ 2 − 1.
一轮 ＝ 1 NADH ＋ 1 FADH₂ ＋ 1 acetyl-CoA，短两个碳。轮数 = 碳数 ÷ 2 − 1。

Fatty acid synthesis is in the cytosol, its committed enzyme is acetyl-CoA carboxylase (biotin, ATP), and it needs a lot of NADPH.
脂肪酸合成在胞质，承诺酶是乙酰-CoA 羧化酶（生物素 ＋ ATP），要大量 NADPH。
横向卡 · 卡 31
Ketone bodies are acetoacetate, β-hydroxybutyrate (the most abundant) and acetone (breathed out). They are made in liver mitochondria, and the committed enzyme is HMG-CoA synthase.
酮体三个：乙酰乙酸 · β-羟丁酸（最多）· 丙酮（可呼出）。在肝线粒体生成，限速酶 HMG-CoA 合成酶。

6 · 生物氧化 四个复合体 · 两个可动载体 · 两类抑制剂
Complex I NADH–ubiquinone oxidoreductase · II succinate–ubiquinone reductase · III ubiquinone–cytochrome c reductase · IV cytochrome c oxidase.
Ⅰ NADH-泛醌还原酶 · Ⅱ 琥珀酸-泛醌还原酶 · Ⅲ 泛醌-细胞色素 c 还原酶 · Ⅳ 细胞色素 c 氧化酶。
卡 25
Complex II is the only one that does not pump protons, and it is where FADH₂ enters. That is why FADH₂ is worth less than NADH.
⚠ 复合体 Ⅱ 是唯一不泵质子的，也是 FADH₂ 进来的地方 —— 所以 FADH₂ 比 NADH 少赚。
卡 25
Ubiquinone is the only non-protein component. The two mobile carriers are ubiquinone and cytochrome c.
泛醌是唯一的非蛋白组分；两个可移动载体 ＝ 泛醌 和 细胞色素 c。
卡 25
Complex IV contains copper and is poisoned by cyanide, carbon monoxide and azide. Rotenone blocks Complex I, antimycin blocks Complex III.
Ⅳ 含铜，被氰化物 · 一氧化碳 · 叠氮剧毒抑制。鱼藤酮堵 Ⅰ，抗霉素堵 Ⅲ。

7 · 氨基酸与氮 脱氨 · 运氨 · 尿素循环
The main route of deamination is transamination coupled to glutamate dehydrogenase: a transaminase moves the amino group onto α-ketoglutarate, and glutamate dehydrogenase then releases it as free ammonia.
最主要的脱氨方式是联合脱氨：转氨酶把氨基交给 α-酮戊二酸 → 谷氨酸脱氢酶再把它放成游离氨。

All transaminases use pyridoxal phosphate, from vitamin B6.
所有转氨酶的辅酶都是磷酸吡哆醛（PLP，维生素 B6）。

Ammonia is carried in non-toxic form: alanine from muscle (the glucose–alanine cycle) and glutamine from brain and other tissues.
氨的无毒运输：肌肉靠丙氨酸（葡萄糖-丙氨酸循环）· 脑和外周靠谷氨酰胺。

8 · DNA 复制 规律 ＋ 六个酶
Replication is semiconservative and semidiscontinuous — the leading strand is continuous, the lagging strand is made as Okazaki fragments. A new strand always grows 5′ → 3′.
半保留 ＋ 半不连续（前导链连续，后随链是冈崎片段）；新链永远 5′→3′ 延伸。
卡 16
Topoisomerase relieves supercoiling by cutting and rejoining. Helicase opens the duplex using ATP. Primase lays down a short RNA primer, because a polymerase cannot start from nothing — it needs a free 3′-OH. Pol III does the main synthesis, Pol I removes the primer and fills the gap, and ligase seals the nick with a phosphodiester bond.
拓扑异构酶解超螺旋（切开再接上）· 解旋酶开链（耗 ATP）· 引物酶放 RNA 引物（聚合酶不能从零起，必须有 3′-OH）· pol Ⅲ 主延伸 · pol Ⅰ 切引物并填补 · 连接酶封口（磷酸二酯键）。
卡 16
Reverse transcriptase has three activities: RNA-dependent DNA polymerase, RNase H, and DNA-dependent DNA polymerase.
逆转录酶三个活性：以 RNA 为模板的 DNA 聚合 · RNase H（降解 RNA）· 以 DNA 为模板的 DNA 聚合。

Repair, five routes: direct reversal · base excision · nucleotide excision · mismatch · double-strand break (HR or NHEJ).
修复五类：直接逆转 · BER · NER · 错配 · 双链断裂（HR／NHEJ）。⚠ 书只写了 UV 嘧啶二聚体被 pol Ⅰ 切除这一句。

9 · 转录 模板链 · σ · 三种聚合酶 · 加工
Transcription reads the template strand, so the RNA has the same sequence as the coding strand, with U in place of T.
以模板链（反义链）为模板，所以 RNA 序列跟编码链（有义链）一致，只是 T 换成 U。

The bacterial holoenzyme is α₂ββ′σ. The σ subunit recognises the promoter and decides where transcription starts; the core enzyme does the elongation.
原核全酶 α₂ββ′σ；σ 亚基识别启动子、决定起始位置，核心酶负责延伸。

真核三种：Ⅰ → rRNA · Ⅱ → mRNA（最常考）· Ⅲ → tRNA 和 5S rRNA。

10 · 翻译 密码子四个性质 ＋ 三步
The start codon is AUG, coding methionine. The three stop codons are UAA, UAG and UGA, and they code for nothing.
起始密码子 AUG（甲硫氨酸）；三个终止密码子 UAA · UAG · UGA，不编码任何氨基酸。
卡 12 · ⚠ 终止密码子 2026-09-27 补
Four properties: degeneracy — one amino acid can have several codons, which softens the damage a mutation does; universality — the same code from viruses to humans; wobble — the third base of the codon pairs loosely with the first base of the anticodon; and it is read without overlap or punctuation.
四个性质：简并性（一个氨基酸多个密码子，减轻突变的伤害）· 通用性（病毒到人共用一套）· 摆动性（密码子第三位与反密码子第一位配对不严格）· 连续无标点阅读。
⚠ 2026-09-27 补
The cycle is entry, peptide bond formation, translocation. The bond is made by peptidyl transferase, which is a ribozyme — an RNA activity of the large subunit.
三步：进位 · 成肽 · 转位。成肽由肽酰转移酶催化，而它是个核酶（大亚基 rRNA 的活性）。
⚠ 2026-09-27 补（核酶这个词卡 18 有）
Energy: activating the amino acid costs ATP; entry and translocation each cost GTP.
耗能：氨基酸活化耗 ATP；进位和转位各耗一个 GTP。
