1 · The ring form — this is the one to learn 环状结构，主要背这个

These five are the ones to learn. Each card leads with the ordinary name; the formal ring name is the small line under it.
这五张是要背的。每张第一行是普通名字，下面小字才是正式的环状名。

In the open chain C1 is a carbonyl, not a chiral centre. Closing the ring makes C1 chiral, and that new centre is the anomeric carbon. So anomers exist only in the ring form.
开链的 C1 是羰基，不是手性中心。一关环，C1 就成了新的手性碳，叫异头碳。所以异头物只有环状结构才有。

Only the OH and CH₂OH groups are drawn; the hydrogens are left out. The thick line at the front, C1 to C4, is the edge nearest you.
图里只画了 OH 和 CH₂OH，氢省略了。前沿那条粗线是离你最近的一边。

2 · The open chain, for reference 开链式 Fischer，备查

You do not have to memorise these. The rings above are what gets drawn and asked about; this open-chain view is here so you can check which carbon is which.
这几张不用硬背 —— 考场上画的、问的都是上面那些环。开链式放在这里只是方便对照哪个碳是哪个。

The vertical line is the carbon chain, C1 on top. The orange position is what differs from glucose. D or L is read off the highest-numbered chiral carbon alone, which for a hexose is C5.
竖着的是碳链，C1 在上。橙色那一处就是和葡萄糖不一样的地方。 D 还是 L 只看编号最大的那个手性碳，己糖就是 C5。

Glucose is right, left, right, right (C2 to C5). Four chiral carbons, so there are 2⁴ ＝ 16 aldohexoses, eight D and eight L.
葡萄糖的 OH 背成四个字：右 · 左 · 右 · 右。四个手性碳 → 己醛糖一共 16 个，D 八个、L 八个。

3 · What an enantiomer looks like 对映异构体

All of the above are D. Every single chiral carbon has to invert to give L-glucose, so it is not an epimer of glucose, it is the mirror image. The body does not metabolise it.
上面那些都是 D 型。把每一个手性碳都翻过来才是 L-葡萄糖 —— 所以它和葡萄糖不是差向异构体，是镜像。人不代谢它。

4 · The pentoses 戊糖

Ribose is right, right, right. The last two are ketoses, the working molecules of the pentose phosphate pathway.
核糖背成三个字：右 · 右 · 右。后面两个是酮糖，磷酸戊糖途径里的主角。

8 · Why some carbons flip directly and some cannot 为什么有的碳能直接翻，有的必须绕

The carbon being flipped has to sit next to the carbonyl.
Next to it: take a proton off, put one back on the other face. No cofactor.
Not next to it: oxidise it to a ketone, turn it over, reduce it back. NAD⁺ required.
要翻的那个碳必须紧挨着羰基。挨着，去质子再加质子就行；不挨着，只能先氧化再还原，必须有 NAD⁺。

⚠ I had this too narrow before. The earlier wording was “only C2 can flip through an enediol”, which is true only for aldoses. A ketose has its carbonyl on C2, so C3 is next to it too — which is exactly why ribulose-5-phosphate 3-epimerase needs no NAD⁺.

9 · The order to answer in 口试答题的顺序

How many carbons differ, and which ones → what mechanism flips them → only then the name.
“They differ at one carbon, carbon four. That carbon is too far from the carbonyl to be flipped directly, so an NAD⁺-dependent epimerase oxidises it and reduces it back. That makes them epimers, and the enzyme is UDP-glucose 4-epimerase.”
先说差几个碳、差的是哪个碳，再说靠什么机制翻过去，最后才报名字。 epimer / isomer / anomer 是结论，不是答案。

Two things to say before you are asked: fructose is not an epimer of glucose (the functional group moved, so it is a constitutional isomer), and L-glucose is not one either (every centre is inverted, so it is an enantiomer).
两个要主动说出口的 ✗：果糖不是葡萄糖的 epimer，L-葡萄糖也不是。
