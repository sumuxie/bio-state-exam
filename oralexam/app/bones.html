Structure & properties

Protein structure

卡 01 · 四层结构，每层靠什么键

Must say

Four levels. Primary is the covalent level, the order of residues. Secondary is held by main-chain hydrogen bonds only, independent of the side chains. Tertiary is held mainly by the hydrophobic effect. Quaternary is several chains, all non-covalent.

二级只靠主链自己的氢键，跟侧链无关——这一句是分水岭。

Numbers

α-helix: 3.6 residues per turn, rise 0.54 nm, hydrogen bond from C=O to the N–H of i+4. Trp and Tyr absorb at 280 nm.

反平行 β 折叠比平行的稳（氢键正对着）。

Hook

Denaturation destroys the higher structure but not the primary structure. Sickle-cell is one residue: Glu → Val at position 6 of the β chain.

Say this

A protein has four levels of structure, and the useful way to remember them is by what holds each one together. The primary structure is the covalent level: the order of residues joined by peptide bonds, including the disulfide positions. The secondary structure is local, and it is held by hydrogen bonds of the main chain only — the side chains take no part. The tertiary structure is the fold of one whole chain, held mainly by the hydrophobic effect, with ionic bonds, hydrogen bonds and disulfides as well. The quaternary structure is several chains together, and it is entirely non-covalent. ⚠ Denaturation destroys everything above the primary level, so the protein loses its activity, but the peptide bonds are not broken.

Proline & glycine — the two odd ones

卡 01 · 几乎必被追问的一对

Must say

Proline is the helix breaker, for two reasons: its nitrogen has no N–H left to donate the hydrogen bond, and its five-membered ring locks one backbone angle. The same two reasons put it in β-turns. Glycine has only a hydrogen as its side chain, so it is the most flexible and the only one with no chiral carbon.

Numbers

On a Ramachandran plot glycine has the largest allowed region and proline the smallest.

拉氏图画的是 φ 和 ψ 两个二面角的允许区。

Hook

Collagen needs a glycine every third residue — only glycine fits the crowded centre of the triple helix.

Say this

Two residues behave differently from the other eighteen. Proline is the helix breaker, for two reasons: its side chain closes back onto its own nitrogen, so there is no N–H left to donate a hydrogen bond, and the five-membered ring locks one of the backbone dihedral angles. Those same two reasons make it common in beta turns, where the chain has to change direction. Glycine is the opposite: its side chain is a single hydrogen, so it is the most flexible residue and the only one without a chiral carbon. On a Ramachandran plot glycine has the largest allowed region and proline the smallest. ⚠ And that flexibility is why collagen needs a glycine at every third position — nothing else fits the centre of the triple helix.

Myoglobin & haemoglobin

卡 01 · 协同效应的教科书例子

Must say

Myoglobin is one subunit and gives a hyperbolic curve — it stores oxygen. Haemoglobin is four subunits and gives a sigmoid curve — it transports it. The sigmoid shape is positive cooperativity: binding the first oxygen pulls the subunits from the T state to the R state.

Numbers

Four subunits, α₂β₂, four oxygens.

Hook

Three things stabilise the T state and make it release oxygen in the tissues: H⁺ and CO₂ (the Bohr effect) and 2,3-BPG, which sits in the positively charged central cavity. Without 2,3-BPG haemoglobin barely releases any oxygen at all.

Say this

Myoglobin has one subunit and a hyperbolic binding curve, and it stores oxygen in muscle. Haemoglobin has four subunits, α₂β₂, and a sigmoid curve, and it transports oxygen. The sigmoid shape means positive cooperativity: binding the first oxygen shifts the subunits from the T state, which binds weakly, to the R state, which binds strongly. Three things stabilise the T state and therefore make haemoglobin release oxygen where it is needed: protons and carbon dioxide, which together are the Bohr effect, and 2,3-bisphosphoglycerate, which sits in the positively charged central cavity. ⚠ Without 2,3-BPG, haemoglobin would hold onto its oxygen and hardly release any in the tissues.

Enzymes & kinetics

卡 02 · 17 · 19

Must say

An enzyme only lowers the activation energy. It does not change the equilibrium constant and it does not change ΔG. The active site has the highest affinity for the transition state, not for the substrate.

「对过渡态亲和力最高」这一句是理解的分水岭，不是背的。

Numbers

Km is the substrate concentration at half Vmax. It is a property of the enzyme, independent of enzyme concentration, and a larger Km means weaker binding. kcat/Km is the specificity constant, limited by diffusion at 10⁸–10⁹ M⁻¹s⁻¹.

Hook

Competitive: Km up, Vmax unchanged, lines meet on the y-axis. Non-competitive: Km unchanged, Vmax down, lines meet on the x-axis. Uncompetitive: both down, lines are parallel. Classic examples: sulfonamides, and malonate on succinate dehydrogenase.

Say this

An enzyme lowers the activation energy and nothing else — it does not change the equilibrium constant and it does not change ΔG, so it only changes how fast equilibrium is reached, not where it lies. ⚠ The key idea is that the active site has its highest affinity for the transition state, not for the substrate, and the binding energy released is what pays for lowering the barrier. Km is the substrate concentration at half Vmax; it is characteristic of the enzyme, independent of enzyme concentration, and a larger Km means weaker binding. For inhibition: competitive raises Km and leaves Vmax, non-competitive lowers Vmax and leaves Km, and uncompetitive lowers both.

Carbohydrates

卡 06

Must say

A monosaccharide is an aldehyde or a ketone with two or more hydroxyls, general formula (CH₂O)ₙ, that cannot be hydrolysed to a smaller sugar.

Numbers

Maltose α-1,4 · lactose β-1,4 · sucrose α-1,β-2 · cellulose β-1,4. Glycogen and starch: main chain α-1,4, branches α-1,6.

Hook

Sucrose is the only non-reducing one of those, because both anomeric carbons are used in the bond. Humans cannot digest cellulose because we have no enzyme for β-1,4.

Say this

A monosaccharide is an aldehyde or a ketone carrying two or more hydroxyl groups, with the general formula (CH₂O)ₙ, and it cannot be hydrolysed to anything smaller. In water it interconverts between the open chain and the ring, which is mutarotation, giving the alpha and beta anomers. The linkages are worth naming precisely: maltose is α-1,4, lactose is β-1,4, sucrose is α-1,β-2, and cellulose is β-1,4. ⚠ Sucrose is the only non-reducing one of those, because both anomeric carbons are used in the bond. Glycogen and starch are both α-1,4 chains with α-1,6 branches, and humans cannot digest cellulose because we have no enzyme for a β-1,4 bond.

Nucleic acid structure

卡 03

Must say

B-DNA is a right-handed double helix with antiparallel strands. A=T is two hydrogen bonds, G≡C is three, so more GC means a more stable helix. Denaturation gives the hyperchromic effect: absorbance at 260 nm rises as the strands separate.

Numbers

10.5 base pairs per turn, 0.34 nm per base. Tm is the temperature at which half is denatured, and it rises with GC content and ionic strength. Pure DNA A260/A280 = 1.8, pure RNA = 2.0.

Hook

Most transcription factors read the sequence through the major groove. The peptidyl transferase activity is in the rRNA — the ribosome is a ribozyme.

Say this

B-DNA is a right-handed double helix with two antiparallel strands, about 10.5 base pairs per turn and 0.34 nanometres per base. A pairs with T through two hydrogen bonds and G with C through three, so a higher GC content gives a more stable helix and a higher melting temperature. When it denatures the strands separate and the absorbance at 260 nanometres rises — that is the hyperchromic effect — and Tm is the temperature at which half of it has melted. ⚠ For purity, pure DNA has an A260 over A280 of 1.8 and pure RNA 2.0. And most transcription factors read the sequence through the major groove, because that is where the bases are accessible.

Metabolism — the ledger

Glycolysis

卡 gly · 十步分两相：前五步花钱，后五步赚钱

  1. 什么时候跑 — 一直在跑 饭后血糖高的时候最快；缺氧的时候它是唯一的能量来源； 红细胞一辈子只能靠它（没有线粒体）。在胞质，不需要氧。
  2. 投入相（第 1–5 步）— 先花 2 个 ATP 磷酸化 → 异构 → 再磷酸化 → 劈开 → 互换 glucose →[hexokinase|ATP] glucose-6-P →[phosphoglucose isomerase] fructose-6-P →[PFK-1|ATP · 限速] fructose-1,6-bisP →[aldolase] DHAP ＋ GAP DHAP 再由 triose phosphate isomerase 变成 GAP，所以一个葡萄糖给出两个 GAP。 ⚠ 为什么一上来要花钱：磷酸基带负电，把葡萄糖锁在细胞里出不去； 而且拉低胞内的游离葡萄糖浓度，进来的梯度才维持得住。
  3. 回收相（第 6–10 步）— 每个三碳赚 2 个 ATP 氧化装磷 → 给 ATP → 挪磷 → 脱水 → 再给 ATP GAP →[GAPDH|NAD⁺ ＋ Pᵢ] 1,3-bisphosphoglycerate →[phosphoglycerate kinase|→ATP] 3-phosphoglycerate →[phosphoglycerate mutase] 2-phosphoglycerate →[enolase|−H₂O] PEP →[pyruvate kinase|→ATP] pyruvate ⚠ GAPDH 是全程唯一的氧化步，它把无机磷装上去做成高能的酰基磷酸， 能量是从这里进来的，后面两次给 ATP 都是靠它。
  4. 三个关卡 — 全是 kinase，全不可逆 hexokinase（肝里是 glucokinase：Km 高、不被 G6P 抑制，饭后才大量吃糖）· PFK-1（最核心：ATP 和柠檬酸关它，AMP、ADP 和果糖-2,6-二磷酸开它）· pyruvate kinase（被果糖-1,6-二磷酸前馈激活；肝型被磷酸化关掉）。
  5. 分叉 — 丙酮酸三条去路，取决于有没有氧 有氧 → 进线粒体，PDH 做成 acetyl-CoA。 缺氧（动物） → 乳酸。缺氧（酵母） → 乙醇 ＋ CO₂。 ⚠ 后两条的目的不是产能，是再生 NAD⁺ —— 没有 NAD⁺，GAPDH 那一步就停， 整条路卡死。这一句被问到的频率很高。
  6. 别的糖从哪儿插进来 果糖（肝里经果糖激酶和醛缩酶 B 进到三碳那一层）· 半乳糖（经三个酶变成 G1P 再进）· 糖原（磷酸解直接给 G1P → G6P，省掉第一个激酶）。
  7. 总账 无氧：净 2 ATP ＋ 2 乳酸（毛 4 净 2）。 有氧：你的书说 38，现代教材 30–32。 ⚠ 差在两处：每个 NADH 折算 3 还是 2.5；以及胞质的两个 NADH 靠哪条穿梭进线粒体 （见呼吸链那一段）—— 这就是 30 和 32 的区别。

Say this

Glycolysis runs in the cytosol and needs no oxygen. It has ten steps in two phases. The first five spend two ATP — phosphorylation, isomerisation, a second phosphorylation, cleavage, and interconversion of the two trioses. The phosphate keeps the sugar inside the cell and keeps the gradient for uptake. The second five recover four, so the net is two ATP and two NADH per glucose. The only oxidation is at glyceraldehyde-3-phosphate dehydrogenase, and that is where the energy enters. Three steps are irreversible and all three are kinases: hexokinase, phosphofructokinase-1, which is the main control point, and pyruvate kinase. Pyruvate then has three fates: with oxygen it goes to acetyl-CoA; without it, to lactate in animals or ethanol in yeast — and the point of that is to regenerate NAD-plus, without which the pathway would stop.

Pentose phosphate pathway

⚠ 她说没碰过的那一批 · 两段，第一段不可逆、第二段可逆

  1. 氧化段 — 两次脱氢，掉一个碳（不可逆）glucose-6-P →[G6PD|NADPH ①] 6-phosphogluconolactone →[lactonase|＋H₂O] 6-phosphogluconate →[6PGDH|NADPH ② · −CO₂] ribulose-5-PG6PD 是限速酶。⚠ 它的信号不是 ATP，是 NADP⁺ 够不够——这条最容易答错。
  2. 非氧化段 — 把糖架重排（可逆）核酮糖-5-磷酸可以异构成核糖-5-磷酸（给核酸），也可以经转酮酶（搬 2 碳）和转醛酶（搬 3 碳）重排回果糖-6-磷酸和甘油醛-3-磷酸，接回糖酵解。⚠ 这一段是可逆的，所以细胞可以按需要决定要核糖还是要 NADPH。

Say this

The pentose phosphate pathway runs in the cytosol and has two parts. The oxidative part is irreversible: two dehydrogenations and one decarboxylation give two NADPH, one CO₂ and ribulose-5-phosphate. The control enzyme is glucose-6-phosphate dehydrogenase, and what controls it is the supply of NADP-plus, not ATP. The non-oxidative part is reversible: transketolase moves two carbons and transaldolase moves three, so the sugars can be rearranged back into fructose-6-phosphate and glyceraldehyde-3-phosphate. The whole pathway neither makes nor spends ATP. Its two products are NADPH, for reductive synthesis and antioxidant defence, and ribose-5-phosphate, for nucleotides.

Pyruvate dehydrogenase

糖和脂之间的单行道 · 三个酶、五个辅因子的接力

  1. 什么时候跑 — 有氧、而且细胞要烧糖的时候 它是糖酵解和柠檬酸循环之间的闸门。能量够了就关，不够就开。 在线粒体基质，丙酮酸得先被转运进去。
  2. 一句话总反应 pyruvate ＋ CoA-SH ＋ NAD⁺ →[pyruvate dehydrogenase complex] acetyl-CoA ＋ CO₂ ＋ NADH 氧化脱羧：掉一个碳变 CO₂，剩两个碳挂到 CoA 的硫上，脱下的氢给 NAD⁺。
  3. 三个酶的接力 E1 脱羧（TPP 上去接住，CO₂ 跑掉）→ E2 把那两个碳经硫辛酰胺的摆臂交给 CoA → E3 收拾残局（把硫辛酰胺重新氧化，氢经 FAD 交给 NAD⁺）。
  4. 五个辅因子 — 四个是 B 族维生素 TPP（B₁）· 硫辛酰胺 · CoA（B₅）· FAD（B₂）· NAD⁺（B₃） 口诀 Tender Loving Care For Nancy。 ⚠ α-酮戊二酸脱氢酶用的是同一套，背一次会两个。
  5. 调控 — 两层，方向一致 产物抑制：acetyl-CoA、NADH、ATP 多了就关。 共价修饰：PDH kinase 磷酸化 → 关；磷酸酶去磷酸化 ＋ Ca²⁺ 和 ADP → 开。 ⚠ 磷酸化＝关，跟糖原磷酸化酶正好相反，这个反向爱考。
  6. 为什么它这么要紧 ⚠ 这一步不可逆，所以 acetyl-CoA 回不到丙酮酸。 糖能变脂肪，脂肪变不回糖，断点就在这儿。 ⚠ 缺硫胺素这一步就瘫，丙酮酸和乳酸堆起来，脑子最先垮（脑只烧糖）。

Say this

Pyruvate dehydrogenase is the gate between glycolysis and the citric acid cycle, in the mitochondrial matrix, and the reaction is an oxidative decarboxylation: pyruvate gives acetyl-CoA, CO₂ and NADH. It is a complex of three enzymes using five cofactors — TPP, lipoamide, coenzyme A, FAD and NAD-plus — and four of those are B vitamins. It is switched off by its own products, acetyl-CoA and NADH, and also by phosphorylation, which is the opposite direction from glycogen phosphorylase. ⚠ And because this step is irreversible, acetyl-CoA can never go back to pyruvate — that is why fat cannot be converted to glucose.

Citric acid cycle

卡 24 · 汇合点，也是原料库 —— 两个身份都要说

  1. 什么时候跑 — 有氧，而且随时 糖、脂、氨基酸都变成 acetyl-CoA 到这里汇合。在线粒体基质。 ⚠ 循环本身不用氧，但离了氧就停 —— 因为 NADH 要靠呼吸链换回 NAD⁺。
  2. 八步 — 中间物是难点，酶名可以现推 acetyl-CoA ＋ oxaloacetate →[citrate synthase] citrate →[aconitase] isocitrate →[isocitrate dehydrogenase|NAD⁺ · −CO₂] α-ketoglutarate α-ketoglutarate →[α-KG dehydrogenase|NAD⁺ · −CO₂] succinyl-CoA →[succinyl-CoA synthetase|GDP＋Pᵢ] succinate →[succinate dehydrogenase|FAD · 内膜上] fumarate →[fumarase|＋H₂O] malate →[malate dehydrogenase|NAD⁺] oxaloacetate 口诀 Can I Keep Selling Sex For Money, Officer?
  3. 碳怎么走 — 进两个，出两个 4 ＋ 2 ＝ 6 → 掉一个碳 5 → 再掉一个 4。 ⚠ acetyl-CoA 和草酰乙酸是「合体」不是「转化」：OAA 是托盘，转一圈还回来，净增 0。 ⚠ 而且第一圈掉的那两个 CO₂ 不是刚进来的那两个碳。
  4. 账 — 一圈四样 2 CO₂ · 3 NADH · 1 FADH₂ · 1 GTP。 换成 ATP：你的书说 12，现代算法 10。 ⚠ 陷阱问法：「循环产多少 ATP」→ 它直接产的只有 1 个 GTP，其余都是呼吸链造的。
  5. 三个关卡 ＋ 刹车 citrate synthase · isocitrate dehydrogenase（最关键）· α-KG dehydrogenase。 三个的刹车都是 NADH —— 所以真正决定转速的是呼吸链跑不跑得动。 Ca²⁺ 开其中两个：肌肉一收缩，钙进来，循环立刻提速。
  6. 特殊的一步 — 琥珀酸脱氢酶 ⚠ 循环里唯一长在内膜上的酶，而且它就是呼吸链复合体 Ⅱ。 它不用 NAD⁺ 用 FAD，被结构类似的丙二酸竞争性抑制。
  7. 两用性 — 它同时是原料库 中间物随时被抽走去做别的：α-酮戊二酸 → 谷氨酸· 草酰乙酸 → 天冬氨酸、糖异生· 琥珀酰-CoA → 血红素· 柠檬酸 → 出线粒体做脂肪酸。 ⚠ 抽走了就得补回来，这叫回补反应（anaplerotic）， 最主要的一个是 pyruvate carboxylase（丙酮酸 ＋ CO₂ → 草酰乙酸）。 这个词书上有（p140）。

Say this

The citric acid cycle runs in the mitochondrial matrix and it is the convergence point — carbohydrate, fat and amino acids all arrive as acetyl-CoA. Each turn gives two CO₂, three NADH, one FADH₂ and one GTP. ⚠ Note that the cycle itself makes only that one GTP directly; everything else is made later by the chain. Three steps are irreversible: citrate synthase, isocitrate dehydrogenase, which is the main control point, and α-ketoglutarate dehydrogenase, and all three are braked by NADH. Succinate dehydrogenase is the only membrane-bound enzyme of the cycle and it is respiratory Complex II. And the cycle is amphibolic: its intermediates are constantly withdrawn for biosynthesis, so they have to be replaced by anaplerotic reactions, mainly pyruvate carboxylase.

Respiratory chain & oxidative phosphorylation

卡 25 · 两件事，只靠质子梯度连着

  1. 什么时候跑 — 只要有氧和还原型载体 前面所有路交上来的 NADH 和 FADH₂ 都在这里兑现。在线粒体内膜上。 ⚠ 真正限制速度的不是氧，是 ADP —— 没有 ADP 可磷酸化，链就慢下来（呼吸控制）。
  2. 电子从哪进 — 两个入口 NADH 从复合体 Ⅰ 进；FADH₂ 从复合体 Ⅱ 进（琥珀酸脱氢酶就是它）。 ⚠ Ⅱ 不泵质子，所以从这儿进的电子少推一段，FADH₂ 才比 NADH 值钱少。
  3. 四个复合体 ＋ 两个搬运工 Ⅰ → Q → Ⅲ → cyt c → Ⅳ → O₂ 辅基：Ⅰ FMN · Ⅱ FAD · Ⅲ b/c₁ · Ⅳ a/a₃＋Cu（口诀 Fat Frogs Bite Ankles）。 ⚠ 泛醌 Q 是唯一的非蛋白组分，脂溶性，带 2 电子 ＋ 2 质子； cyt c 是内膜外侧的外周蛋白，一次只带 1 个电子。
  4. 泵出来多少质子 现代：Ⅰ 泵 4 ＋ Ⅲ 泵 4 ＋ Ⅳ 泵 2 ＝ 每个 NADH 10 个；FADH₂ 只走后两段，6 个。 你的书：Ⅳ 不算泵，每个 NADH 6 个 ≈ 3 ATP，FADH₂ 4 个 ≈ 2 ATP。 ⚠ 两套都要会，而且要说得出差在哪。
  5. ATP 合酶 — 一台被质子推着转的机器 F₀ 是嵌在膜里的质子通道，质子流过去让它转；F₁ 是伸进基质的头， 转动改变三个催化位点的构象，把 ADP ＋ Pᵢ 挤成 ATP。 ⚠ 每个 ATP 要 4 个质子：3 个用来转，第 4 个用来把磷酸运进基质。 这就是 10 ÷ 4 ＝ 2.5 的来历。
  6. 胞质的 NADH 怎么进来 — 两条穿梭，而且值钱不一样 NADH 本身过不了内膜，糖酵解造的那两个得换个方式进去： 苹果酸-天冬氨酸穿梭（肝、心）→ 在基质里还原出 NADH，值 2.5； 甘油磷酸穿梭（肌肉、脑）→ 交给 FAD，变成 FADH₂，只值 1.5。 ⚠ 这就是「一个葡萄糖 30 还是 32」的答案 —— 看用哪条穿梭。
  7. 毒物三类 — 分清楚才不会答反 ① 堵复合体：鱼藤酮 Ⅰ · 抗霉素 Ⅲ · 氰化物 / CO / 叠氮 Ⅳ → 链停，ATP 也停。 ② 解偶联（DNP、褐色脂肪的 UCP）：质子漏回去 → ATP 停，但链跑得更快、耗氧更多，能量变热。 ③ 堵合酶（寡霉素）：质子出不去、淤积 → ATP 和链一起停。 ⚠ ②和③的对比是最常考的一对。

Say this

The respiratory chain and ATP synthesis are two separate things, coupled only through the proton gradient — that is chemiosmosis, and no chemical intermediate passes between them. Electrons from NADH enter at Complex I and from FADH₂ at Complex II, which pumps no protons, and that is why FADH₂ is worth less. They pass along ubiquinone, Complex III, cytochrome c and Complex IV to oxygen. On the modern count, ten protons are pumped per NADH and six per FADH₂, and the synthase needs four protons per ATP — three to turn it and one to bring the phosphate in — which gives 2.5 and 1.5. My own textbook counts six protons and about three ATP instead. ⚠ Cytosolic NADH cannot cross the membrane, so it enters by a shuttle: the malate–aspartate shuttle delivers NADH and the glycerol phosphate shuttle delivers FADH₂, and that is why a glucose is worth thirty or thirty-two depending on the tissue.

Say this

Fatty acids are the body’s main fuel, especially when fasting. When blood glucose falls, insulin drops and glucagon rises, hormone-sensitive lipase breaks down the stored triacylglycerol, and the free fatty acids travel to the tissues bound to albumin. ⚠ The brain cannot use them, which is exactly why the liver turns them into ketone bodies. Beta-oxidation itself happens in the mitochondrial matrix, and there are two things to get through first. The fatty acid must be activated to a thioester with coenzyme A, which costs two high-energy bonds because the ATP goes all the way to AMP. Then the acyl group has to cross the inner membrane on carnitine, and carnitine acyltransferase I is the rate-limiting step of the whole pathway — it is inhibited by malonyl-CoA, so switching synthesis on switches oxidation off. The cycle itself is four steps: oxidation, hydration, oxidation and thiolysis, giving one FADH₂, one NADH and one acetyl-CoA per round, and the chain is two carbons shorter each time. An even-numbered chain ends as acetyl-CoA. An odd-numbered one leaves propionyl-CoA, which is carboxylated and then rearranged by a B12-dependent mutase into succinyl-CoA — and that is the only part of a fatty acid that can become glucose. Very long chains, above about twenty-two carbons, are shortened in the peroxisome first, where the first oxidation gives hydrogen peroxide instead of FADH₂, so that part yields heat rather than ATP.

Say this

Fatty acid synthesis happens in four stages. First, the carbon has to leave the mitochondrion. Acetyl-CoA cannot cross the inner membrane, so it comes out as citrate, and ATP-citrate lyase splits it back in the cytosol. Say this

Both storage fat and membrane phospholipids are built after a meal, when insulin is high, and they share the same beginning. The backbone is glycerol-3-phosphate, and two fatty acids are esterified onto it to give phosphatidic acid. That is the branch point. Seven of the steps are simply glycolysis running backwards, because those steps are near equilibrium. Three steps are irreversible and need four different enzymes. Glycogen

Say this

Glycogen synthesis needs the glucose activated first, as UDP-glucose. Then glycogen synthase makes the α-1,4 chain and a branching enzyme makes the α-1,6 branches. In the digestive tract it is hydrolysis, by amylases. Only the liver and kidney have glucose-6-phosphatase, so muscle glycogen can never raise blood glucose. And the two directions share one cascade: phosphorylation activates the phosphorylase and inactivates the synthase, so the cell never runs both at once.

Amino acid breakdown separates the nitrogen from the carbon skeleton. The nitrogen is collected by transamination onto glutamate — the transaminases all need pyridoxal phosphate, vitamin B6 — and then released as free ammonia by glutamate dehydrogenase. Because ammonia is toxic it travels to the liver as alanine from muscle or as glutamine from other tissues. In the liver the urea cycle runs with its first two steps in the mitochondrion and the last three in the cytosol. The control enzyme is carbamoyl phosphate synthetase I, switched on by N-acetylglutamate. The two nitrogens of urea come from different places: one from free ammonia, one from aspartate. It costs four ATP per urea, although the summary equation writes three, because one ATP is broken all the way to AMP.

Photosynthesis is the biological conversion of radiant energy into chemical energy, and it happens in two halves in two places. The electrons pass through plastoquinone, cytochrome b₆f, plastocyanin and photosystem I to ferredoxin and NADP reductase, giving NADPH, while the protons pumped into the lumen drive an ATP synthase — the same machine as in the mitochondrion. The fixation of CO₂ runs in the stroma in three stages: carboxylation by rubisco, reduction, and regeneration, and it spends exactly the ATP and NADPH the light reactions made. ⚠ The oxygen comes from water, not from carbon dioxide, and my textbook makes that point by writing twelve waters rather than six.

Must say

Hook

Three cross-pathway signals are worth more than any list: citrate shuts down glycolysis and opens fat synthesis; malonyl-CoA shuts down fat oxidation; acetyl-CoA shuts down PDH and opens gluconeogenesis.

Say this

I would answer this as four questions: where control sits, how it acts, what the cell senses, and who gives the order. Where: only at steps that are far from equilibrium, because at a near-equilibrium step the forward and reverse rates are both large, so adding enzyme speeds up both and the net flux barely moves. Say this

The tissues do not all eat the same thing, and the differences are the point. Post-translational modification

卡 13 · 06 · 基因只写了序列，功能是修饰出来的（⚠ 这一节原来一页纸上没有）

  1. 什么时候跑 — 翻译当中和翻译之后 一条刚合成的肽链还不是成品。折叠、切割、加基团之后才有功能。 ⚠ 一个基因因此能给出好几种不同的蛋白。

Say this

A newly made polypeptide is not yet a finished protein, and post-translational modification is why one gene can give several different products. Where a protein is modified depends first on where it is sent, and that is decided by a signal peptide: a short hydrophobic stretch at the N-terminus that is recognised by SRP as soon as it emerges, so the ribosome is brought to the ER membrane and the chain is threaded into the ER as it is being made. The signal peptide is then cut off. And that is exactly why N-glycosylation can be co-translational. N-glycosylation begins in the ER and is completed in the Golgi. The core sugar chain is pre-assembled on a lipid carrier called dolichol, and while the protein is still entering the ER that whole chain is transferred onto the amide nitrogen of an asparagine, at the sequence Asn–X–Ser or Thr, where X can be anything except proline. O-glycosylation is different: it happens in the Golgi, the sugars are added one at a time onto the hydroxyl oxygen of serine or threonine, and there is no consensus sequence. The most common regulatory modification is phosphorylation, on the hydroxyls of serine, threonine and tyrosine, and it is reversible, which is what makes it a switch. ⚠ And practically: E. coli does not glycosylate, so a protein that needs its sugars has to be expressed in a eukaryotic host.

⚠ 散在各条路里、单独看都很小，但合起来只有七个 —— 它们回答的是同一个问题：膜挡住了这个分子，细胞怎么把它弄过去

  7. 葡萄糖-丙氨酸循环 — 同一条路，顺便把氮也捎上 肌肉里丙酮酸接上一个氨基变成丙氨酸再送去肝； 肝把氨基摘下来进尿素循环，碳架去糖异生。 ⚠ 一趟车运两样东西：Cori 循环只运碳，这一条碳和氮一起运。

Say this

Several pathways are split across a membrane, and the cell always solves that the same way: it converts the molecule into something that can cross, and converts it back on the other side. Acetyl-CoA leaves the mitochondrion as citrate for fatty acid synthesis, and the return leg also generates NADPH. Fatty acyl groups go the other way on carnitine, and carnitine acyltransferase I is the rate-limiting step, inhibited by malonyl-CoA — so the two shuttles are interlocked. Cytosolic NADH cannot cross either: the malate–aspartate shuttle delivers NADH and is worth 2.5 ATP, while the glycerol phosphate shuttle delivers FADH₂ and is worth only 1.5, which is why a glucose is worth thirty or thirty-two depending on the tissue. Between organs, the Cori cycle sends lactate from muscle to the liver and glucose back, and the glucose–alanine cycle does the same journey but carries the amino nitrogen as well.

  4. 延伸 — 领头连续，随从断成冈崎片段 模板反平行，而新链只能 5′→3′，所以一条能顺着叉一直走（领头链）， 另一条只能倒着一段一段做（随从链）。原核主力是 pol Ⅲ： α 亚基聚合 · ε 亚基 3′→5′ 校对 · β 亚基是滑动夹（把酶箍在模板上，决定持续合成能力）。
  5. 收尾 — 换引物、封口 pol Ⅰ 用它的 5′→3′ 外切酶把 RNA 引物切掉、同时用聚合酶活性补上 DNA； 连接酶把缺口接成磷酸二酯键。
  6. 保真 — 三道关，各降几个数量级 碱基配对约 10⁻⁴–10⁻⁵ → 校对再降 2–3 个数量级 → 错配修复再降。 ⚠ 错配修复怎么认出哪条是新链？细菌靠 GATC 的半甲基化（新链还没来得及甲基化）， 真核靠新链上的缺口。
  7. 修复五类 — Don't Be Nervous, Mum's Driving 直接逆转（光复活酶拆 UV 二聚体）· BER（糖苷酶挖单个碱基 → AP 位点 → AP 内切酶）· NER（大块损伤两侧各一刀；⚠ 坏了得着色性干皮病）· MMR · 双链断裂（HR 用姐妹染色单体、经 Holliday junction，准；NHEJ 直接接，快但出错）。 ⚠ 书上只有半句（p079：pol Ⅰ 切除 UV 嘧啶二聚体再补缺口）—— 那就是切除修复，书只是没给名字。先说书那句，再挂通用名。
  8. 真核多出来的麻烦 — 末端 线性染色体的随从链末端，引物一拆就缺一小段，每复制一次短一点。 端粒是那段可牺牲的重复序列，端粒酶（自带 RNA 模板的逆转录酶）能补回去 —— 大多数体细胞不表达它，干细胞和癌细胞表达。

Say this

Replication is semiconservative and semidiscontinuous, and the reason for the second one is that the new strand can only grow 5′ to 3′. Helicase opens the duplex, single-strand binding protein holds it open, and topoisomerase relieves the supercoiling ahead of the fork. Because DNA polymerase cannot start from nothing, primase lays down a short RNA primer. Pol III then extends — continuously on the leading strand, in Okazaki fragments on the lagging strand — with its ε subunit proofreading and its β subunit acting as a sliding clamp. Pol I replaces the primers and ligase seals the nicks. Accuracy comes in three layers: base pairing, proofreading, and mismatch repair, and mismatch repair knows which strand is new because the new strand is not yet methylated.

约 55 秒 · 「只能 5′→3′，所以随从链断成片」是这段的因果

Transcription

横向卡 x-trans · 不需要引物，这是跟复制最大的差别

  1. 什么时候跑 — 需要这个基因的产物时 ⚠ 转录不需要引物，RNA 聚合酶能从零开始。以 DNA 的模板链（反义链）为模板， 所以新 RNA 的序列跟编码链一致，只是 T 换成 U。
  2. 起始 — σ 亚基认门牌 原核全酶是 α₂ββ′σ。⚠ σ 不参与催化，它唯一的任务是认出启动子、定下起点； 起始一旦迈出第一步，σ 就脱落，剩下核心酶 α₂ββ′ 负责延伸。
  3. 延伸和终止 核心酶沿模板走，边走边解链、边走边让后面回粘。 终止有两种：不依赖 ρ 的（RNA 自己形成发夹 ＋ 一串 U 就掉下来）和依赖 ρ 的（ρ 因子追上去把它拽下来）。
  4. 真核 — 三套聚合酶各管一摊 Pol Ⅰ → rRNA（在核仁）· Pol Ⅱ → mRNA（最常考）· Pol Ⅲ → tRNA 和 5S rRNA。 ⚠ 记法「一老二新三转运」。鹅膏蕈碱专门毒 Pol Ⅱ。
  5. 加工 — 真核 mRNA 要做三件事 5′ 加帽（m⁷G，防外切酶降解 ＋ 帮核糖体识别）· 3′ 加 polyA 尾（不依赖模板，维持稳定）· 剪接（剪接体切掉内含子、拼上外显子）。 ⚠ 可变剪接让一个基因能出好几种蛋白 —— 这是真核复杂性的来源之一。
  6. 原核对真核，一句话对比 原核：转录和翻译同时进行（没有核膜隔开），mRNA 不加工、多顺反子。 真核：先在核里转录和加工，再运出去翻译，单顺反子。

Say this

Transcription needs no primer, which is the main difference from replication. It reads the template strand, so the RNA matches the coding strand with U in place of T. In bacteria the holoenzyme is α₂ββ′σ, and the sigma subunit does not catalyse anything — its only job is to recognise the promoter, and it falls off once initiation has happened. Eukaryotes have three polymerases: I makes rRNA, II makes mRNA, and III makes tRNA and 5S. Eukaryotic mRNA is then processed in three ways: a 5′ cap, a poly-A tail, and splicing out the introns. And in bacteria, because there is no nucleus, transcription and translation happen at the same time.

约 45 秒 · 「不需要引物」和「σ 认门牌然后脱落」是两个抓手

Translation

横向卡 x-trans · 每个残基烧四个高能键，贵得有道理

  1. 什么时候跑 — 有 mRNA 就跑 遗传密码的四个性质：简并性（一个氨基酸可以有好几个密码子，让很多突变不致命）· 通用性（从病毒到人共用一套）· 摆动性 · 无标点、不重叠。 起始 AUG（甲硫氨酸），终止 UAA · UAG · UGA（不编码任何氨基酸）。
  2. 活化 — 先把氨基酸装到 tRNA 上 amino acid ＋ tRNA ＋ ATP →[aminoacyl-tRNA synthetase] aminoacyl-tRNA ＋ AMP ＋ PPᵢ ⚠ ATP 断成 AMP，所以这一步就花掉 2 个高能键。 ⚠ 这个酶是密码正确性的真正把关人：它必须同时认对氨基酸和tRNA， 认错了后面核糖体发现不了。
  3. 起始 — 小亚基先找到起点 原核靠 mRNA 上的 Shine-Dalgarno 序列跟 16S rRNA 配对定位； 真核靠 5′ 帽，小亚基从帽子开始扫描到第一个 AUG。 然后大亚基合上，起始 tRNA 坐进 P 位。
  4. 延伸 — 三个动作循环 进位：EF-Tu 护送氨酰-tRNA 进 A 位，花 1 GTP → 成肽：肽酰转移酶把 P 位的肽链接到 A 位的氨基酸上（⚠ 这个活性在 rRNA 上，核糖体是核酶，不花 GTP）→ 转位：EF-G 推着核糖体沿 mRNA 走一个密码子，再花 1 GTP。
  5. 摆动 — 为什么 tRNA 不用 61 种 tRNA 反密码子的第 1 位跟密码子的第 3 位配对时可以松一点： C 只配 G · A 只配 U · U 配 A 或 G · G 配 C 或 U · 次黄嘌呤 I 能配 A、C、U。 ⚠ 所以一个 tRNA 能认好几个密码子，细胞不需要 61 种。
  6. 终止 — 没有 tRNA 认终止密码子 释放因子（蛋白质，不是 tRNA）进 A 位，让肽酰转移酶把肽链水解下来。
  7. 能量账 — 每个残基 4 个高能键 活化 2（ATP→AMP）＋ 进位 1 GTP ＋ 转位 1 GTP ＝ 4。 ⚠ 为什么这么贵：翻译是全细胞最耗能的过程之一，代价买的是精确 —— 一个错的氨基酸可能毁掉整条蛋白。

Say this

Translation starts at AUG and stops at UAA, UAG or UGA, and the code is degenerate, universal and read with wobble. The amino acid is first attached to its tRNA by an aminoacyl-tRNA synthetase, and because the ATP goes to AMP that costs two high-energy bonds. Elongation is then three steps repeated: EF-Tu brings the tRNA into the A site using one GTP, peptidyl transferase makes the bond — and that activity is in the rRNA, so the ribosome is a ribozyme — and EF-G translocates using another GTP. So each residue costs four high-energy phosphate bonds, and what that buys is accuracy. Termination is done by release factors, which are proteins, not tRNAs.

约 45 秒 · 「4 个高能键」和「核糖体是核酶」是两个必说

The lac operon

卡 12

Must say

Two switches, and both must be right. The repressor is the negative one, cAMP–CAP is the positive one.

Numbers

Three states: no lactose → off; lactose and glucose → barely on, because glucose keeps cAMP low; lactose and no glucose → fully on.

Hook

Glucose lowers cAMP, so the cell will not bother with lactose while there is glucose. That is catabolite repression.

Say this

The lac operon has two switches, and both have to be right. The repressor is the negative one: without lactose it sits on the operator and blocks the polymerase. cAMP with CAP is the positive one: it recruits the polymerase, but only when cAMP is high. So there are three states. With no lactose, the repressor is bound and the operon is off. With lactose and glucose, the repressor comes off, but glucose keeps cAMP low, so there is only weak basal transcription. With lactose and no glucose, the repressor is off and cAMP is high, so the operon is fully on. ⚠ That last effect of glucose is called catabolite repression — the cell will not bother with lactose while there is glucose to eat.

约 40 秒 · 「两个开关都要对」是这题的形状

Cloning, PCR & sequencing

卡 pe-clone · taq · 你自己天天做的那一套

  1. 为什么要克隆 — 要拿到很多份同一个基因 把目的基因放进一个能在细菌里自主复制的载体，细菌一分裂就替你复制一遍。
  2. 质粒三件套 Ori（复制起点，能自主复制）· 选择标记（抗性基因，在平板上毒死没转进去的）· MCS（多克隆位点，一小段集中了很多独特酶切位点）。
  3. 把基因放进去 — 两条路 传统：限制性内切酶切出黏末端，连接酶接上。 无切法：restriction-free cloning / Gibson —— 用带同源臂的引物把插入片段变成一条大引物， ⚠ 不需要合适的酶切位点，这是它最大的好处。
  4. 送进细胞 ＋ 挑出来 转化（化学感受态 ＋ 热激）或电穿孔。 铺抗生素平板 → 只有带质粒的长得出来 → 挑单克隆 → 测序确认。
  5. PCR — 三步一轮，指数放大 变性 → 退火 → 延伸。⚠ Taq 没有 3′→5′ 校对，Kapa 和 Pfu 有 —— 要克隆、要表达的，必须用有校对的那种，不然错配会被一路放大。
  6. Sanger 测序 — 靠一个缺掉的羟基 反应里掺一点 ddNTP：它的糖环 3′ 位是 −H 而不是 −OH。 一旦被掺进去，下一个磷酸二酯键再也建不起来，链当场停住。 不同长度的片段按大小分开，就读出了序列。

Say this

A cloning vector needs three things: an origin of replication so it copies itself in the host, a selectable marker so that only the cells that took it up survive, and a multiple cloning site. The insert can be put in with restriction enzymes and ligase, or by restriction-free cloning, where the insert itself is used as a large primer, which avoids needing suitable restriction sites at all. PCR is denaturation, annealing and extension, and Taq has no 3′→5′ proofreading while Kapa and Pfu do, so anything that will be cloned should be amplified with a proofreading polymerase. Sanger sequencing works by chain termination with ddNTPs: the 3′ position is a hydrogen instead of a hydroxyl, so no further phosphodiester bond can form.
