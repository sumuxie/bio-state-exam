Structure & properties

Protein structure

卡 01 · 四层结构，每层靠什么键

Must say

Four levels. Primary is the covalent level, the order of residues. Secondary is held by main-chain hydrogen bonds only, independent of the side chains. Tertiary is held mainly by the hydrophobic effect. Quaternary is several chains, all non-covalent.

二级只靠主链自己的氢键，跟侧链无关——这一句是分水岭。

Numbers

α-helix: 3.6 residues per turn, rise 0.54 nm, hydrogen bond from C=O to the N–H of i+4. Trp and Tyr absorb at 280 nm.

反平行 β 折叠比平行的稳（氢键正对着）。

Hook

Denaturation destroys the higher structure but not the primary structure. Sickle-cell is one residue: Glu → Val at position 6 of the β chain.

Proline & glycine — the two odd ones

卡 01 · 几乎必被追问的一对

Must say

Proline is the helix breaker, for two reasons: its nitrogen has no N–H left to donate the hydrogen bond, and its five-membered ring locks one backbone angle. The same two reasons put it in β-turns. Glycine has only a hydrogen as its side chain, so it is the most flexible and the only one with no chiral carbon.

Numbers

On a Ramachandran plot glycine has the largest allowed region and proline the smallest.

拉氏图画的是 φ 和 ψ 两个二面角的允许区。

Hook

Collagen needs a glycine every third residue — only glycine fits the crowded centre of the triple helix.

Myoglobin & haemoglobin

卡 01 · 协同效应的教科书例子

Must say

Myoglobin is one subunit and gives a hyperbolic curve — it stores oxygen. Haemoglobin is four subunits and gives a sigmoid curve — it transports it. The sigmoid shape is positive cooperativity: binding the first oxygen pulls the subunits from the T state to the R state.

Numbers

Four subunits, α₂β₂, four oxygens.

Hook

Three things stabilise the T state and make it release oxygen in the tissues: H⁺ and CO₂ (the Bohr effect) and 2,3-BPG, which sits in the positively charged central cavity. Without 2,3-BPG haemoglobin barely releases any oxygen at all.

Enzymes & kinetics

卡 02 · 17 · 19

Must say

An enzyme only lowers the activation energy. It does not change the equilibrium constant and it does not change ΔG. The active site has the highest affinity for the transition state, not for the substrate.

「对过渡态亲和力最高」这一句是理解的分水岭，不是背的。

Numbers

Km is the substrate concentration at half Vmax. It is a property of the enzyme, independent of enzyme concentration, and a larger Km means weaker binding. kcat/Km is the specificity constant, limited by diffusion at 10⁸–10⁹ M⁻¹s⁻¹.

Hook

Competitive: Km up, Vmax unchanged, lines meet on the y-axis. Non-competitive: Km unchanged, Vmax down, lines meet on the x-axis. Uncompetitive: both down, lines are parallel. Classic examples: sulfonamides, and malonate on succinate dehydrogenase.

Carbohydrates

卡 06

Must say

A monosaccharide is an aldehyde or a ketone with two or more hydroxyls, general formula (CH₂O)ₙ, that cannot be hydrolysed to a smaller sugar.

Numbers

Maltose α-1,4 · lactose β-1,4 · sucrose α-1,β-2 · cellulose β-1,4. Glycogen and starch: main chain α-1,4, branches α-1,6.

Hook

Sucrose is the only non-reducing one of those, because both anomeric carbons are used in the bond. Humans cannot digest cellulose because we have no enzyme for β-1,4.

Nucleic acid structure

卡 03

Must say

B-DNA is a right-handed double helix with antiparallel strands. A=T is two hydrogen bonds, G≡C is three, so more GC means a more stable helix. Denaturation gives the hyperchromic effect: absorbance at 260 nm rises as the strands separate.

Numbers

10.5 base pairs per turn, 0.34 nm per base. Tm is the temperature at which half is denatured, and it rises with GC content and ionic strength. Pure DNA A260/A280 = 1.8, pure RNA = 2.0.

Hook

Most transcription factors read the sequence through the major groove. The peptidyl transferase activity is in the rRNA — the ribosome is a ribozyme.

Metabolism — the ledger

Glycolysis

卡 gly · 你已经碰过的三条之一

Must say

In the cytosol, and it does not need oxygen. Three steps are irreversible and all three are kinases: hexokinase, phosphofructokinase-1 — the main control point, and pyruvate kinase.

Numbers

Net 2 ATP and 2 NADH per glucose — four made, two spent.

⚠ 有氧时一个葡萄糖：你的书说 38，现代教材 30–32。被问先问有没有氧，再给两个数。

Hook

Red cells have no mitochondria, so this is all they have. Without oxygen pyruvate is reduced to lactate, and the point of that is to regenerate NAD⁺.

Pentose phosphate pathway

⚠ 她说没碰过的那一批 · 两段，第一段不可逆、第二段可逆

  1. 氧化段 — 两次脱氢，掉一个碳（不可逆）glucose-6-P →[G6PD|NADPH ①] 6-phosphogluconolactone →[lactonase|＋H₂O] 6-phosphogluconate →[6PGDH|NADPH ② · −CO₂] ribulose-5-PG6PD 是限速酶。⚠ 它的信号不是 ATP，是 NADP⁺ 够不够——这条最容易答错。
  2. 非氧化段 — 把糖架重排（可逆）核酮糖-5-磷酸可以异构成核糖-5-磷酸（给核酸），也可以经转酮酶（搬 2 碳）和转醛酶（搬 3 碳）重排回果糖-6-磷酸和甘油醛-3-磷酸，接回糖酵解。⚠ 这一段是可逆的，所以细胞可以按需要决定要核糖还是要 NADPH。

Say this

The pentose phosphate pathway runs in the cytosol and has two parts. The oxidative part is irreversible: two dehydrogenations and one decarboxylation give two NADPH, one CO₂ and ribulose-5-phosphate. The control enzyme is glucose-6-phosphate dehydrogenase, and what controls it is the supply of NADP-plus, not ATP. The non-oxidative part is reversible: transketolase moves two carbons and transaldolase moves three, so the sugars can be rearranged back into fructose-6-phosphate and glyceraldehyde-3-phosphate. The whole pathway neither makes nor spends ATP. Its two products are NADPH, for reductive synthesis and antioxidant defence, and ribose-5-phosphate, for nucleotides.

Pyruvate dehydrogenase

糖和脂之间的单行道

Must say

An oxidative decarboxylation in the mitochondrial matrix, and it is irreversible. Pyruvate plus CoA plus NAD⁺ gives acetyl-CoA, CO₂ and NADH.

这一步不可逆，就是「脂肪变不回糖」的断点。

Numbers

Five cofactors: TPP, lipoamide, CoA, FAD and NAD⁺ — four of them are B vitamins.

α-酮戊二酸脱氢酶用同一套，背一次会两个。

Hook

Thiamine deficiency stalls this step, so pyruvate and lactate accumulate. The brain suffers first because it runs on glucose. Phosphorylation switches it off — the opposite of glycogen phosphorylase.

Citric acid cycle

卡 24 · 你已经碰过的三条之一

Must say

The convergence point — sugars, fats and amino acids all arrive here as acetyl-CoA. Three irreversible steps: citrate synthase, isocitrate dehydrogenase (the main one) and α-ketoglutarate dehydrogenase.

⚠ acetyl-CoA 跟草酰乙酸是合体，不是「变成」——OAA 是托盘，转一圈还回来。

Numbers

One turn: 3 NADH, 1 FADH₂, 1 GTP and 2 CO₂.

换成 ATP：书 12，现代 10。两个都要会。

Hook

Succinate dehydrogenase is the only membrane-bound enzyme of the cycle, and it is respiratory Complex II. It is competitively inhibited by malonate.

陷阱：「循环产多少 ATP」→ 它直接产的只有 1 个 GTP，其余是呼吸链造的。

Respiratory chain & oxidative phosphorylation

卡 25 · 你已经碰过的三条之一

Must say

Chemiosmosis: no chemical intermediate passes between the chain and the synthase, they are coupled only through the proton gradient. Complex II pumps nothing, which is why FADH₂ is worth less.

Numbers

Modern count: 4 + 4 + 2 = 10 protons per NADH, and 4 protons per ATP, giving 2.5 for NADH and 1.5 for FADH₂.

⚠ 你的书算的是 6 个质子 ≈ 3 ATP。先给书的数，再说现代值差在哪。

Hook

Cyanide, CO and azide block Complex IV. An uncoupler such as DNP stops ATP synthesis but speeds the chain up and turns everything into heat. Oligomycin blocks the synthase and stops both.

这两个的对比比任何一个单独记都值钱。

Fatty acid oxidation

⚠ 她说太短的那一段，补全了 · 三步进门，一个循环，三个分叉

  1. 活化 — 在胞质，先做成硫酯 fatty acid ＋ CoA ＋ ATP →[acyl-CoA synthetase] acyl-CoA ＋ AMP ＋ PPᵢ ⚠ ATP 断成 AMP，不是 ADP，焦磷酸随后又被水解掉 —— 所以这一步的代价是 两个高能磷酸键，不是一个。书说得很明白：游离脂肪酸进不了任何代谢通路，合成和分解都得先活化。
  2. 肉碱穿梭 — 真正的限速门 长链酰基-CoA 过不了线粒体内膜。外膜上的 CPT-1 把酰基换到肉碱上， translocase 把它换进去，内侧的 CPT-2 再换回 CoA。 ⚠ CPT-1 是整条路的限速步，而它被丙二酰-CoA 关掉 —— 那是脂肪酸合成的第一个产物。 合成一开，氧化的门就锁上。⚠ 中短链脂肪酸不用这道门，直接就进去了。
  3. 四步循环 — 氧 · 水 · 氧 · 切 acyl-CoA →[acyl-CoA dehydrogenase|FAD] trans-Δ²-enoyl-CoA →[enoyl-CoA hydratase|＋H₂O] 3-hydroxyacyl-CoA →[3-hydroxyacyl-CoA dehydrogenase|NAD⁺] 3-ketoacyl-CoA →[thiolase|＋CoA] acetyl-CoA ＋ acyl-CoA（短两个碳） 每轮出 1 FADH₂ ＋ 1 NADH ＋ 1 acetyl-CoA，轮数 ＝ 碳数 ÷ 2 − 1。 ⚠ 白拿的加分：前三步跟柠檬酸循环最后三步是同一套化学（脱氢 → 加水 → 再脱氢）。
  4. 分叉一 · 奇数碳 — 最后剩三个碳 偶数碳链切到最后正好是两个 acetyl-CoA，干净利落。 奇数碳链最后一轮剩下的是丙酰-CoA（3 碳），它得另走一条路： propionyl-CoA →[propionyl-CoA carboxylase|biotin · ATP] methylmalonyl-CoA →[methylmalonyl-CoA mutase|B₁₂] succinyl-CoA ⚠ 琥珀酰-CoA 是柠檬酸循环的中间物，能变成草酰乙酸，所以能做糖。 这是脂肪酸里唯一生糖的那一小块 —— 而「脂肪不能变成糖」的大原则还是对的， 因为那说的是偶数碳链给出的 acetyl-CoA。 ⚠ 顺带：这里是 B₁₂ 在代谢里露面的两处之一。
  5. 分叉二 · 超长链（C > 22）— 先去过氧化物酶体 线粒体处理不了太长的链。C22 以上的先在过氧化物酶体里切几轮，短了再交给线粒体。 ⚠ 那里的第一步不一样：用的是氧化酶，电子直接交给氧， 产物是 H₂O₂ 而不是 FADH₂（过氧化氢酶随后把它拆掉）。 所以那几轮不产 ATP，能量变成热。 ⚠ 病名知道就行：X 连锁肾上腺脑白质营养不良（超长链进不了过氧化物酶体）。
  6. 分叉三 · 不饱和 — 要多两个酶 天然双键是顺式，而循环只认反式 Δ²。 所以双键在奇数位时要一个异构酶把它搬正；在偶数位时还要一个 还原酶（用 NADPH）先把两个双键并成一个再搬。 ⚠ 每绕过一个双键，就少一次 FAD 脱氢，所以不饱和脂肪酸产的 ATP 略少。
  7. 总账 — 一整条棕榈酸（C16） 7 轮 → 8 acetyl-CoA ＋ 7 FADH₂ ＋ 7 NADH，再减去活化花掉的 2 个高能键。 现代算法 ≈ 106 ATP；按你书的折算率（acetyl-CoA 12、NADH 3、FADH₂ 2）≈ 129。 ⚠ 报数之前先说是按哪套算的。

Say this

Beta-oxidation happens in the mitochondrial matrix, and there are two things to get through first. The fatty acid must be activated to a thioester with coenzyme A, which costs two high-energy bonds because the ATP goes all the way to AMP. Then the acyl group has to cross the inner membrane on carnitine, and carnitine acyltransferase I is the rate-limiting step of the whole pathway — it is inhibited by malonyl-CoA, so switching synthesis on switches oxidation off. The cycle itself is four steps: oxidation, hydration, oxidation and thiolysis, giving one FADH₂, one NADH and one acetyl-CoA per round, and the chain is two carbons shorter each time. An even-numbered chain ends as acetyl-CoA. An odd-numbered one leaves propionyl-CoA, which is carboxylated and then rearranged by a B12-dependent mutase into succinyl-CoA — and that is the only part of a fatty acid that can become glucose. Very long chains, above about twenty-two carbons, are shortened in the peroxisome first, where the first oxidation gives hydrogen peroxide instead of FADH₂, so that part yields heat rather than ATP.

Fatty acid synthesis

⚠ 她点名模糊的那一条 · 四步，第一步就是把碳运出线粒体

  1. Citrate shuttle — 把碳运出线粒体acetyl-CoA 在线粒体里，合成在胞质，它过不了内膜。于是跟草酰乙酸缩合成柠檬酸坐转运体出来，胞质里 ATP-citrate lyase 花一个 ATP 再把它劈回 acetyl-CoA。⚠ 草酰乙酸回程走「苹果酸 → malic enzyme → 丙酮酸」，顺手造出一部分 NADPH，其余的从磷酸戊糖途径来。
  2. Acetyl-CoA carboxylation — 承诺步骤acetyl-CoA ＋ HCO₃⁻ ＋ ATP →[acetyl-CoA carboxylase|biotin] malonyl-CoA限速步，调控全压在这个酶上：柠檬酸开它 · 棕榈酰-CoA 关它 · AMPK 磷酸化关它 · 胰岛素开它。⚠ 而且 malonyl-CoA 反手关掉 CPT-1——合成一开，氧化的门就锁上。
  3. Fatty acid synthase complex — 真正长链子的地方动物的 FAS 是一条多功能肽链，摆臂是 ACP（那条臂来自泛酸）。每轮四步：缩合（同时放掉一个 CO₂）→ 还原（NADPH）→ 脱水 → 再还原（NADPH），链子长两个碳。⚠ 必考的怪事：第 2 步加上的 CO₂ 在这里又放掉了，根本没进产物——它是用来买反应的，脱羧释放的能量推着缩合往前走，换来这一步不可逆。
  4. 后加工 — 加长和加双键FAS 只做到 C16 棕榈酸，转七轮之后硫酯酶把它切下来。更长的链和双键都在内质网另外加。⚠ 人做不出 C9 以后的双键，所以亚油酸和亚麻酸是必需脂肪酸。

Say this

Fatty acid synthesis happens in four stages. First, the carbon has to leave the mitochondrion. Acetyl-CoA cannot cross the inner membrane, so it comes out as citrate, and ATP-citrate lyase splits it back in the cytosol. The oxaloacetate returns as pyruvate, and that return trip makes some of the NADPH. Second is the committed step: acetyl-CoA carboxylase uses biotin and one ATP to make malonyl-CoA. Third is the fatty acid synthase complex. Each round is condensation, reduction, dehydration, reduction, it spends two NADPH, and the chain grows by two carbons. Seven rounds give palmitate. Fourth, anything longer or unsaturated is added afterwards in the ER. Overall: eight acetyl-CoA, seven ATP and fourteen NADPH per palmitate.

Triacylglycerol & phospholipid synthesis

⚠ 脂质合成的另一半 —— 造好的脂肪酸接下来去哪儿

Must say

Both start from the same backbone: glycerol-3-phosphate gets two fatty acids to make phosphatidic acid. From there the path forks. Remove the phosphate and add a third fatty acid and you get a triacylglycerol, for storage. Keep the phosphate and put a polar head group on it — choline, ethanolamine, serine — and you get a glycerophospholipid, for membranes.

同一个岔路口：磷脂酸 → 去磷酸＝储能脂肪，留磷酸＋极性头＝膜脂。

Numbers

A triacylglycerol is glycerol plus three fatty acids by ester bonds, completely non-polar. A phospholipid is amphipathic — that is why one stores and the other forms bilayers.

Hook

Sphingolipids do not use glycerol at all — the backbone is sphingosine, and the fatty acid is attached through an amide bond, not an ester.

Cholesterol

⚠ 脂质合成的第三块

Must say

Every carbon of cholesterol comes from acetyl-CoA. The committed and rate-limiting step is HMG-CoA reductase, making mevalonate.

Numbers

Cholesterol is the sterol class: a rigid four-fused-ring core. It is what tunes membrane fluidity in animals, and the precursor of steroid hormones, bile acids and vitamin D.

Hook

Statins are competitive inhibitors of HMG-CoA reductase. And note that HMG-CoA is the branch point: in the mitochondrion it goes to ketone bodies, in the cytosol to cholesterol.

Vitamins & coenzymes

⚠ 2026-09-27 她点名：完全没碰过 · 卡 31

Must say

The structural idea is one sentence: a water-soluble vitamin is the part of a coenzyme the body cannot make. So you do not memorise vitamins — you memorise which coenzyme each becomes, and what that coenzyme carries.

这一句是整章的钥匙。不是背维生素，是背「它变成哪个辅酶、那个辅酶搬什么」。

Numbers

B₁ thiamine → TPP, carries an aldehyde (the decarboxylations). B₂ riboflavin → FAD / FMN, carries hydrogen. B₃ niacin → NAD⁺ / NADP⁺, carries hydride. B₅ pantothenate → coenzyme A, carries acyl groups. B₆ pyridoxine → PLP, carries amino groups. B₇ biotin carries CO₂. B₉ folate → THF, carries one-carbon units. B₁₂ cobalamin carries methyl groups. Vitamin C is the odd one out — not a coenzyme, but the reductant that keeps the iron of collagen hydroxylases working.

⚠ 两套名字都要认：考官可能说 B₁，也可能说 thiamine，还可能只说 TPP——同一样东西的三个叫法。
B₃ 还有个老名字 vitamin PP（也叫尼克酸／烟酰胺）；B₇ 偶尔被写成 vitamin H。
口诀 Naked Rats Take Pictures Before Party ＝ Niacin · Riboflavin · Thiamine · Pantothenate · Biotin · Pyridoxine。

Hook

Water-soluble ones are coenzyme parts; the fat-soluble ones, A D E K, are not. They act as a light receptor, a hormone, an antioxidant and a clotting cofactor. Deficiencies worth the name only: thiamine → beriberi, niacin → pellagra, vitamin C → scurvy (collagen cannot be hydroxylated), vitamin D → rickets, vitamin K → bleeding.

⚠ A D E K 脂溶性，其余水溶性 —— 这个分界本身就是一道题。

Say this

A water-soluble vitamin is the part of a coenzyme that the body cannot make, so each one is worth remembering as a pair: which coenzyme it becomes, and what that coenzyme carries. B one, thiamine, becomes TPP, which carries an aldehyde. B two, riboflavin, becomes FAD, which carries hydrogen. B three, niacin, becomes NAD-plus, and the split matters — NADH goes to the respiratory chain, NADPH goes to biosynthesis. B five, pantothenate, becomes coenzyme A, carrying acyl groups. B six, pyridoxine, becomes PLP, carrying amino groups. B seven is biotin, which carries CO₂. B nine, folate, becomes tetrahydrofolate, carrying one-carbon units. And B twelve carries methyl groups. The fat-soluble ones, A, D, E and K, are not coenzyme parts, and because they are stored rather than excreted, A and D can be toxic in excess.

约 55 秒 · 「辅酶里身体造不出来的那一截」是开口第一句 · ⚠ 编号和名字一起说，两套都能接得上

Who carries what

七个基团载体 —— 把上面那张维生素表反过来查

Must say

Every activated group travels on a dedicated carrier. Acyl on CoA · CO₂ on biotin · amino on PLP · one carbon on tetrahydrofolate · methyl on SAM · aldehyde on TPP · sugar on UDP.

Can Bears Play Tennis? Some Try Upstairs.

Numbers

Two electron carriers, and the split is the whole point: NADH goes to the respiratory chain to make ATP; NADPH goes to biosynthesis.

Hook

Several of these carriers contain a nucleotide that takes no part in the chemistry — it is a handle for the enzyme to grip, not cargo.

Ketone bodies

⚠ 没碰过的一条 · 肝内生成、肝外利用

  1. 什么时候造 — acetyl-CoA 多到循环吃不下饥饿或糖尿病时脂肪大量分解，草酰乙酸又被抽去做糖异生，于是 acetyl-CoA 进不了柠檬酸循环，只能改道。
  2. 生成（肝线粒体）2 acetyl-CoA →[thiolase] acetoacetyl-CoA →[HMG-CoA synthase|限速] HMG-CoA →[HMG-CoA lyase] acetoacetate乙酰乙酸再被还原成 β-羟丁酸（血里最多的那个），或者自发脱羧成丙酮。⚠ 丙酮是废气不是燃料，从呼吸里出去。
  3. 利用（肝外）β-hydroxybutyrate →[BDH|NAD⁺] acetoacetate →[thiophorase (SCOT)|借 succinyl-CoA 的 CoA] acetoacetyl-CoA →[thiolase] 2 acetyl-CoA⚠ 肝自己用不了，因为肝没有 thiophorase——这一句解释了为什么肝造了却全送走。
  4. 谁在吃心和肌肉平时就吃；脑正常只吃葡萄糖，长期饥饿才改用酮体。⚠ 红细胞永远不能用——没有线粒体。

Say this

Ketone bodies are made in the liver and used outside it. They are made when acetyl-CoA is produced faster than the citric acid cycle can take it, in starvation and in diabetes. There are three: acetoacetate, β-hydroxybutyrate and acetone, but only the first two are fuel — acetone is waste and is breathed out. The committed enzyme is HMG-CoA synthase, in the liver mitochondrion. The tissues that use them convert them back to acetyl-CoA with thiophorase, and the liver cannot use them itself precisely because it lacks that enzyme. The heart and muscle use them routinely; the brain only after prolonged starvation, and red cells never, because they have no mitochondria.

Gluconeogenesis

⚠ 没碰过的一条 · 七步共用，四个酶绕过三道坎

  1. 第一道坎 — 丙酮酸 → PEP，要两个酶两步走pyruvate →[pyruvate carboxylase|biotin · ATP · 线粒体] oxaloacetate →[PEP carboxykinase|GTP · −CO₂] PEP⚠ 先加一个 CO₂ 再把它拿掉，跟脂肪酸合成那一步是同一个把戏：花钱买不可逆。而且 acetyl-CoA 开启 pyruvate carboxylase——脂肪够烧了就去造糖。
  2. 中间七步 — 直接借糖酵解的酶倒着跑这七步本来就近平衡，浓度一变方向就反，不需要另外的酶。
  3. 第二道坎 — 果糖-1,6-二磷酸酶fructose-1,6-bisP →[fructose-1,6-bisphosphatase|水解，不是逆激酶] fructose-6-P⚠ 被果糖-2,6-二磷酸关掉，而同一个分子开启 PFK-1——一个分子管住正反两条路。
  4. 第三道坎 — 葡萄糖-6-磷酸酶glucose-6-P →[glucose-6-phosphatase|肝 · 肾] glucose⚠ 只有肝和肾有这个酶，所以只有它们能把葡萄糖放进血里。肌肉没有。

Say this

Gluconeogenesis makes glucose from lactate, glycerol and glucogenic amino acids, mainly in the liver and kidney. Seven of the steps are simply glycolysis running backwards, because those steps are near equilibrium. Three steps are irreversible and need four different enzymes. Pyruvate is carboxylated to oxaloacetate by pyruvate carboxylase, using biotin and ATP, and then PEP carboxykinase spends GTP to make phosphoenolpyruvate. Fructose-1,6-bisphosphatase and glucose-6-phosphatase then hydrolyse the two phosphates off. The cost is four ATP, two GTP and two NADH per glucose, and what that cost buys is direction.

约 40 秒 · 四个酶的名字 ＋ 4/2/2 这个代价

Glycogen

⚠ 没碰过的一条 · 合成和分解各四步，共用一套开关

  1. 合成 — 先活化，再接上去glucose-6-P →[phosphoglucomutase] glucose-1-P →[UDP-glucose pyrophosphorylase|＋UTP] UDP-glucose →[glycogen synthase|α-1,4] glycogen⚠ 葡萄糖不能直接接上去，必须先做成 UDP-葡萄糖。分支由分支酶做成 α-1,6。
  2. 分解 — 细胞内是磷酸解，不是水解glycogen →[glycogen phosphorylase|＋Pᵢ · α-1,4] glucose-1-P →[phosphoglucomutase] glucose-6-P⚠ 用磷酸而不是水去切，所以出来的是磷酸糖，省掉一次激酶。分支点由脱支酶处理，那里放出的是游离葡萄糖。⚠ 消化道里才是水解（淀粉酶），这个二分是考点。
  3. 出口 — 只有肝能放糖进血glucose-6-P →[glucose-6-phosphatase|只有肝和肾] glucose⚠ 肌肉没有这个酶，所以肌糖原只给肌肉自己用。
  4. 调控 — 一套级联，两个相反的结果磷酸化之后：磷酸化酶开（b→a），合成酶关（a→b）。所以肾上腺素和胰高血糖素一来，拆的开、造的关，绝不会同时跑——这就是避免无效循环。

Say this

Glycogen synthesis needs the glucose activated first, as UDP-glucose. Then glycogen synthase makes the α-1,4 chain and a branching enzyme makes the α-1,6 branches. Breakdown inside the cell is not hydrolysis but phosphorolysis: glycogen phosphorylase uses inorganic phosphate, so the product is already glucose-1-phosphate. In the digestive tract it is hydrolysis, by amylases. Only the liver and kidney have glucose-6-phosphatase, so muscle glycogen can never raise blood glucose. And the two directions share one cascade: phosphorylation activates the phosphorylase and inactivates the synthase, so the cell never runs both at once.

约 40 秒 · 磷酸解 vs 水解、肌肉没有磷酸酶，两句必说

Amino acid breakdown & the urea cycle

⚠ 没碰过的一条 · 氮和碳分开处理

  1. 摘氨基 — 转氨，再集中脱氨各种氨基酸把氨基交给 α-酮戊二酸，变成谷氨酸（转氨酶，辅酶 PLP，来自 B6）；谷氨酸再由谷氨酸脱氢酶放出游离氨。这两步合起来叫联合脱氨。
  2. 把氨运到肝 — 两条运输线肌肉走丙氨酸-葡萄糖循环，脑和其他组织走谷氨酰胺。⚠ 游离氨有毒，绝不在血里裸奔。
  3. 尿素循环 — 两步在线粒体，三步在胞质NH₄⁺ ＋ HCO₃⁻ →[CPS I|2 ATP · 限速] carbamoyl phosphate →[ornithine transcarbamoylase] citrullinecitrulline →[argininosuccinate synthetase|＋aspartate · ATP→AMP] argininosuccinate →[argininosuccinate lyase|−fumarate] arginine →[arginase] urea ＋ ornithine⚠ 两个氮来路不同：一个是游离氨，一个由天冬氨酸带进来。CPS I 要 N-乙酰谷氨酸才开工——有活干才开机。
  4. 碳骨架去哪剩下的碳架按去向分两类：生糖的进丙酮酸或柠檬酸循环的中间物，生酮的变 acetyl-CoA。⚠ 纯生酮只有两个：Leu 和 Lys。

Say this

Amino acid breakdown separates the nitrogen from the carbon skeleton. The nitrogen is collected by transamination onto glutamate — the transaminases all need pyridoxal phosphate, vitamin B6 — and then released as free ammonia by glutamate dehydrogenase. Because ammonia is toxic it travels to the liver as alanine from muscle or as glutamine from other tissues. In the liver the urea cycle runs with its first two steps in the mitochondrion and the last three in the cytosol. The control enzyme is carbamoyl phosphate synthetase I, switched on by N-acetylglutamate. The two nitrogens of urea come from different places: one from free ammonia, one from aspartate. It costs four ATP per urea, although the summary equation writes three, because one ATP is broken all the way to AMP.

约 50 秒 · 两个氮的来路 ＋ 4 对 3 那个说破

Photosynthesis

卡 27 · 28

Must say

Two halves in two places. The light reactions run in the thylakoid membrane and give ATP, NADPH and oxygen. The fixation of CO₂ runs in the stroma and spends exactly those two.

同一个细胞器的两个隔间，跟线粒体「膜上发电、旁边花钱」是同一个故事。

Numbers

12 H₂O ＋ 6 CO₂ →[light · pigments] C₆H₁₂O₆ ＋ 6 O₂ ＋ 6 H₂O, ΔG°′ = +2867 kJ/mol.

正号 ＝ 吸能，这就是为什么非要有光。

Hook

The oxygen comes from water, not from carbon dioxide. The book writes twelve waters instead of six precisely to make that point. And rubisco is the most abundant enzyme on Earth.

Metabolic regulation

卡 32 · 四问

Must say

Four questions: where control sits, how it acts, what the cell senses, who gives the order. Control sits only at the steps that are far from equilibrium, because only there does the enzyme set the net flux.

Numbers

A reaction is near equilibrium when Q and K′eq are within one or two orders of magnitude — true of more than half the enzymes of glycolysis.

Hook

Three cross-pathway signals are worth more than any list: citrate shuts down glycolysis and opens fat synthesis; malonyl-CoA shuts down fat oxidation; acetyl-CoA shuts down PDH and opens gluconeogenesis.

Who eats what

器官分工 —— 常被当作串场题

Must say

The liver is the switchboard: after a meal it stores, in starvation it releases glycogen, runs gluconeogenesis and makes ketone bodies for everyone else.

Numbers

The brain normally burns only glucose and takes about 20% of the oxygen; only in prolonged starvation does it switch to ketone bodies.

Hook

Red cells have no mitochondria — glycolysis only, and that is also why they depend on the pentose phosphate pathway for NADPH.

Replication & repair

卡 16

Must say

Semiconservative and semidiscontinuous, and the new strand always grows 5′ to 3′. Primase lays an RNA primer, pol III extends, pol I replaces the primer, ligase seals.

Numbers

Three layers of accuracy: base pairing gives about one error in 10⁴–10⁵, proofreading improves that by two to three orders of magnitude, and mismatch repair improves it again.

Hook

Five repair routes: direct reversal, base excision, nucleotide excision, mismatch repair, and double-strand break repair. Failure of nucleotide excision repair gives xeroderma pigmentosum.

⚠ 书上有半句：p079 说 pol Ⅰ 的 5′→3′ 外切酶切除 UV 二聚体再补缺口——那就是切除修复，书只是没给名字。先说书那句，再挂通用名。

Transcription

横向卡 x-trans

Must say

Transcription needs no primer and reads the template strand, so the RNA matches the coding strand with U for T. In bacteria the sigma subunit only recognises the promoter and falls off once initiation has happened.

Numbers

Three eukaryotic polymerases: I makes rRNA, II makes mRNA, III makes tRNA and 5S.

Hook

Eukaryotic mRNA gets three things done to it: a 5′ cap, a poly-A tail, and splicing by the spliceosome.

Translation

横向卡 x-trans

Must say

Start at AUG, stop at UAA, UAG or UGA. The peptide bond is made by peptidyl transferase, and that activity is in the rRNA, so the ribosome is a ribozyme.

Numbers

Four high-energy phosphate bonds per residue: two to activate the amino acid, one GTP to bring it in, one GTP to translocate.

Hook

Wobble: the first base of the anticodon pairs loosely with the third base of the codon. Inosine is the flexible one — it can read A, C or U.

简并性让很多突变不致命；摆动性让一个 tRNA 认好几个密码子。

The lac operon

卡 12

Must say

Two switches, and both must be right. The repressor is the negative one, cAMP–CAP is the positive one.

Numbers

Three states: no lactose → off; lactose and glucose → barely on, because glucose keeps cAMP low; lactose and no glucose → fully on.

Hook

Glucose lowers cAMP, so the cell will not bother with lactose while there is glucose. That is catabolite repression.

Cloning & sequencing

卡 pe-clone · taq · 实验基本技术

Must say

A plasmid needs three things: an origin so it replicates, a selectable marker so you can kill the cells that did not take it, and a multiple cloning site.

Numbers

Sanger sequencing works by chain termination with ddNTPs: the 3′ position is –H instead of –OH, so no further phosphodiester bond can form.

Hook

Taq has no 3′→5′ proofreading; Kapa and Pfu do. If the product is going to be cloned and expressed, use the one that proofreads.
